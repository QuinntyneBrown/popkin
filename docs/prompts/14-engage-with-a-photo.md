# Feature: Engage with a photo (journey 14 of `docs/journeys.md`)

## Goal

In the photo view, a member reacts with one of five quick reactions or more, opens the comments to read and add them, saves the photo at its original size, and deletes their own photo (the Host can delete any).

**Journey:** 14. Engage with a photo: "Member reacts to, comments on and saves a photo." Estimated at 3 days in `docs/journeys.md`.

**Before you start:** Journeys 1 to 13 are merged; the album, the photo view and photos in chat exist.

**In scope:**

- Photo reactions with counts, pressed state, toggling and failure rollback (L2-034).
- The Photo comments dialog in its default, empty and busy states, with a 500-character limit and delete of one's own after confirming (L2-035).
- Save photo at the size it was added, delete one's own or (Host) any after confirming, and the effects of deletion on the album, chat messages, reactions and comments (L2-036).

**Stays unchanged:**

- Adding and browsing photos.
