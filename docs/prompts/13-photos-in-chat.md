# Feature: Photos in chat (journey 13 of `docs/journeys.md`)

## Goal

A member chooses Photo in the composer, picks or takes one photo, sees it above the message field with Remove photo, and sends it with or without text. The photo shows in the thread at 4:3, at most 16 rem wide, opens full screen, and, in Everyone, is also added to Photos under the sender's name.

**Journey:** 13. Photos in chat: "Member attaches photos to a chat message." Estimated at 2 days in `docs/journeys.md`.

**Before you start:** Journeys 1 to 12 are merged; the photo store, the album and the photo view exist.

**In scope:**

- Attaching, previewing, removing and sending one photo per message (L2-026 AC1, AC4).
- Showing the photo in the message and opening it full screen (L2-026 AC2).
- Adding Everyone photos to the album and keeping direct-message photos out of it (L2-026 AC3).
- The same in direct messages (L2-023 AC3 for photos).

**Stays unchanged:**

- The album, add-photos and photo view behaviour, which are reused.
