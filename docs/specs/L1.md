# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Each L1 traces to L0-001, the mission. `docs/specs/L0.md` is not written yet; until it is, the
purpose statement in `AGENTS.md` stands in for it and nothing here restates it.

## Source and assumptions

These requirements are inferred from the Popkin Community Platform designs (26 screens exported
from Magic Patterns) and from the defaults of the requirements skill (production grade, security
first, fast, responsive, testable). Where the designs are silent, the requirement states an
assumption. The assumptions are:

- The six-digit code: how long it lasts, how many attempts it allows, how often it can be resent
  (L2-004, L2-005).
- Every length and size limit: names, messages, captions, comments, photo files (L2-008, L2-021,
  L2-031, L2-040).
- What a person who is "keen" on an experience does to say so (L2-042). The designs show only the
  count.
- Popkin settings, leaving a Popkin, account settings, signing out and deleting an account
  (L2-020, L2-047, L2-048, L2-049). The designs show the entry points only.
- The wording and timing of rate limits, session length and performance budgets (L1-012, L1-013).
- The wide layout of the screens the designs do not draw: sign in, the code screens, join with a
  code, the account screen and the full-page errors (L2-058).

## Roles

- **Visitor**: someone without a session. Sees the landing page, the welcome screen and an
  invite.
- **Member**: a signed-in person who belongs to a Popkin. Sees and changes only the Popkins they
  belong to.
- **Host**: the member who started a Popkin. Does everything a member does, and also changes the
  Popkin's name, emoji, colour and length, and wraps it up.

## Terms

- **Popkin**: a private space for one group, with a name, an emoji, a colour, members and a
  length.
- **Colour**: one of seven accents a Popkin carries: Coral, Teal, Sky, Berry, Leaf, Plum, Amber.
- **Invite**: the QR code, invite link or code that lets a person join one Popkin.
- **Everyone**: the chat thread that every member of a Popkin shares.
- **Direct message**: a chat thread between two members of the same Popkin.
- **Group call**: a video call open to every member of a Popkin.
- **Photos**: the Popkin's shared album.
- **Experience**: something the group did, a restaurant, or something the group wants to do, which
  members rate and comment on.
- **Wrap up**: to end a Popkin. A wrapped-up Popkin is read-only and has a recap.
- **Recap**: the summary of a wrapped-up Popkin: its people, messages, photos and top-rated
  moment.

## L1-001: Public landing page

**Traces to:** L0-001

A visitor must be able to learn what Popkin is, how it works, what it is used for and that it is
free, and start a Popkin or sign in, from a public page that needs no account.

## L1-002: Accounts without passwords

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to create an account and sign in with only a name and a phone number or an
email address, confirmed by a one-time six-digit code. The system must never ask for or store a
password. A person must be able to change their name and photo, sign out and delete their
account.

## L1-003: Start a Popkin

**Traces to:** L0-001

A signed-in person must be able to start a Popkin in about a minute by giving it a name, an emoji
and a colour and choosing how long it lasts, and must receive its invite the moment it exists.

## L1-004: Invite and join

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to invite people with a QR code or an invite link, without knowing their
phone number. An invited person must be able to see which Popkin they are joining and who invited
them, join it with a name and an optional photo, and be welcomed in.

## L1-005: My Popkins

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to see every Popkin they belong to, active and wrapped up, with what is new
in each, and open any of them, start a new one or scan to join another.

## L1-006: Popkin home

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to see one Popkin at a glance: who is in it, how long it has left, the
latest chat, photos and experiences, and reach chat, photos, experiences, a group call and the
invite from it. The Host must be able to change the Popkin's name, emoji, colour and length.

## L1-007: Chat

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to talk in a thread everyone shares and in direct messages
between two members, with text, photos, emoji reactions and replies, and see new messages without
reloading.

## L1-008: Calls

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to start or join a group video call with one tap, call one
member from a direct message, and control their own microphone and camera.

## L1-009: Photos

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to add photos to a shared album, browse it by day, view a photo
with its caption, react to it, comment on it and save it.

## L1-010: Experiences

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to share experiences (things the group did, restaurants, and
things the group wants to do), rate them from one to five stars, comment on them and see the
group's score.

## L1-011: Wrap up and recap

**Traces to:** L0-001

A Popkin must end when its length runs out or when its Host wraps it up. A wrapped-up Popkin must
become read-only, give every member a recap, and keep or remove its photos as the Host chose.

## L1-012: Privacy and security

**Traces to:** L0-001

A Popkin must be private to its members. The system must authenticate every request, authorise it
against membership and role, validate every input, protect personal data in transit and at rest,
limit abuse and resist the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-013: Performance and reliability

**Traces to:** L0-001

The system must respond quickly on a phone over a mobile network, deliver messages in near real
time, and tell people plainly when something is loading, has failed or is offline, without losing
what they typed.

## L1-014: Responsive, accessible and themed

**Traces to:** L0-001

Every screen must work on every viewport from 360 px wide upward and use the width it is given,
with touch on a phone and with the keyboard on a desktop, in a light and a dark theme, and must meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## L1-015: Free

**Traces to:** L0-001

Popkin must be free to use: no payment, no premium tier and no advertising anywhere in it.
