# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Each L1 traces to L0-001, the mission. `docs/specs/L0.md` is not written yet; until it is, the
purpose statement in `AGENTS.md` stands in for it and nothing here restates it.

## Source and assumptions

These requirements are inferred from the Popkin Community Platform designs (26 screens exported
from Magic Patterns) and from the defaults of the requirements skill (production grade, security
first, fast, responsive, testable). Where the designs are silent, the requirement states an
assumption. The assumptions are:

- The six-digit code: how long it lasts, how many attempts it allows, how often it can be resent
  (L2-004, L2-005).
- Every length and size limit: names, messages, captions, comments, photo files (L2-008, L2-021,
  L2-031, L2-040).
- What a person who is "keen" on an experience does to say so (L2-042). The designs show only the
  count.
- Popkin settings, leaving a Popkin, account settings, signing out and deleting an account
  (L2-020, L2-047, L2-048, L2-049). The designs show the entry points only.
- The wording and timing of rate limits, session length and performance budgets (L1-012, L1-013).
- The wide layout of the screens the designs do not draw: sign in, the code screens, join with a
  code, the account screen and the full-page errors (L2-058).
- A Popkin's address: its form and length, the reserved words, that it never changes and is never
  given to another Popkin, and that it opens the Popkin for its members only and admits nobody
  (L2-062 to L2-067). The designs do not draw it.
- Plan and Schedules (L1-017, L1-018; L2-068 to L2-075) come from the Plan Board, Burn-down,
  Time off and This week designs, which draw one Popkin of six people planning a week away. The
  designs and the requirements call the first tab "Plan". A Popkin can gather for any kind of
  event, and a trip is only one example of them, so no requirement names a feature, a screen or a
  concept "trip"; the owner set this rule. The designs are silent on: the length limit of a
  story's title and of a tag; who may delete a story; where a member's working hours come from
  (the design says "from everyone's work calendars"; the requirement has members enter their own
  hours, and a calendar connection is an open question); the limit on how far ahead time off
  can be entered; what the event of time off is. The owner settled the event: the Host sets its
  label and days from "Popkin settings" (L2-085).
- The admin app (L1-019, L1-021 to L1-028; L2-076 to L2-078, L2-086 to L2-120) comes from the
  "Popkin Admin" designs (Magic Patterns) and their mocks, `docs/mocks/admin/`. What a member sees
  when a feature is off is an assumption. The owner settled four questions on 15 November 2026:
  - **Moderation.** An admin sees what a member reported, with the messages around it, and no
    other content. For a serious case an Owner or Support admin may open one Popkin's Everyone
    thread, album and experiences, read-only, by giving a reason the audit log records. A direct
    message is seen only when one of its two members reports it. The Privacy notice says so.
  - **Admins.** Configuration names the first Owner; Owners add and remove admins and change
    their role in the app. There are three roles: Owner, Support and Viewer.
  - **The Popkin app's side.** Members report a message, a photo or an experience, and see the
    announcement banner; both are in the Popkin app.
  - **Features.** Each feature is Off, on for chosen Popkins, or on for everyone.

  The rest are assumptions: what each role may do is the designs' table (L2-086); the codes and
  lockout of the admin sign-in; how many messages around a report are shown and how long a
  moderation view lasts; that the audit log is kept two years; and which limits can be changed.
  Where the designs contradict a requirement already written (the message and photo limits,
  what happens to the Popkins a deleted account hosts), the requirement stands and the mock
  changes.
- The marketing site (L1-020; L2-079 to L2-081) is drawn by its own mock,
  `docs/mocks/pages/marketing-home/default.html`, made from the landing page's mock and the pitch
  film "Meet Popkin" (`docs/videos/19-meet-popkin/`). Its address, `about.popkin.community`, is an
  assumption (ADR 0017).
- Contacts (L1-029 to L1-038; L2-121 to L2-151) is written from its mocks (`contacts-*`,
  `contact-*`, `add-contact`, `log-entry` and their dialogs and toasts), which are drawn from the
  Contacts prototype in the Popkin Community Platform OS. The owner settled on 15 November 2026
  that Contacts is specified as drawn, phone notifications and the Monday email included. Where
  the mocks are silent, nothing is assumed: no requirement is written and the gap is open in
  `docs/detailed-designs/README.md` (Known gaps, 99 to 118). Where two mocks disagreed, the
  requirement takes one reading and the mock was changed to match: a wedding's reminder, the
  label of a birthday, a milestone's yearly nudge, a habit's rhythm, the Clubs section of
  Groups, a couple's connection, where a promise came from, the date reminder's toast, and
  pronouns, since a contact has none and is named instead.

## Roles

- **Visitor**: someone without a session. Sees the landing page, the welcome screen and an
  invite. Anyone, with a session or without, sees the marketing site.
- **Member**: a signed-in person who belongs to a Popkin. Sees and changes only the Popkins they
  belong to.
- **Host**: the member who started a Popkin. Does everything a member does, and also changes the
  Popkin's name, emoji, colour and length, and wraps it up.
- **Admin**: a person who runs Popkin itself, from the admin app, a separate application. Every
  admin has one role:
  - **Owner**: does everything, including the admins, the features, the limits and the
    announcements.
  - **Support**: helps people and Popkins and moderates content.
  - **Viewer**: reads everything and changes nothing.

  An admin sees a Popkin's content only as moderation allows (L1-024); inside the Popkin app an
  admin is an ordinary person.

Any signed-in person also has their own Contacts (L1-029), which only they see. A contact is not
told they are in it, and Contacts gives no admin and no other person a view of it.

## Terms

- **Popkin**: a private space for one group, with a name, an emoji, a colour, members and a
  length.
- **Colour**: one of seven accents a Popkin carries: Coral, Teal, Sky, Berry, Leaf, Plum, Amber.
- **Address**: the web address that belongs to one Popkin, `https://{address}.popkin.community`,
  where `{address}` is a short word the Host chose when starting it, such as `lakeside-weekend`.
- **Invite**: the QR code, invite link or code that lets a person join one Popkin.
- **Everyone**: the chat thread that every member of a Popkin shares.
- **Direct message**: a chat thread between two members of the same Popkin.
- **Group call**: a video call open to every member of a Popkin.
- **Photos**: the Popkin's shared album.
- **Experience**: something the group did, a restaurant, or something the group wants to do, which
  members rate and comment on.
- **Plan**: the tab where a Popkin plans what it is getting together for, whatever kind of event
  that is. It holds the **plan board** and the **burn-down**.
- **Plan board**: the Popkin's shared plan: stories in four columns, Backlog, To do, Doing and
  Done.
- **Story**: one job someone can own. It has a title, a column, a size in points, a tag, and
  optionally an owner and a due date.
- **Points**: the size of a story, one of 1, 2, 3, 5 or 8. "1 is a quick text. 8 is a weekend of
  phone calls."
- **Tag**: what a story is about: Travel, Stay, Food, Money or Fun.
- **Sprint**: the stretch of days the Popkin gives itself to get the plan done, with a name and
  a start and end date. The burn-down is drawn over it.
- **Burn-down**: the chart of points left per day of the sprint against the ideal line, with
  whether the group is behind, ahead or on track and when it will finish at this week's pace.
- **Schedule**: the tab where a Popkin sees when its people are free. It holds **time off** and
  **this week**.
- **Event**: what a Popkin is getting together for, with a label and a first and last day, set by
  the Host when Schedules is on. A Popkin may have none.
- **Time off**: each member's days off around the group's event, week by week: Off, Tentative
  (planned, not yet confirmed with work) or Working.
- **This week**: each member's working hours over the next seven days and the evenings everyone
  is free.
- **Feature switch**: an on/off setting an admin controls for one feature across every Popkin.
  Plan and Schedules each have one.
- **Admin app**: the separate application, at `https://admin.popkin.community`, where admins
  sign in and run Popkin. Its screens call it "the console".
- **Production**: the label the admin app always shows, naming the system it changes.
- **Reason**: what an admin writes when they change something, kept in the audit log beside the
  change.
- **Audit log**: the record of every change an admin makes: who, what, to whom or what, why and
  when. Nobody can edit it.
- **Report**: a member's word that a message, photo or experience breaks the rules, with one of
  five reasons: Harassment, Spam or advertising, Shows a child's face without consent, Shares
  private information, Inappropriate.
- **Moderation view**: an Owner's or Support admin's read-only look at one Popkin's Everyone
  thread, album and experiences, opened with a reason and closed after 30 minutes.
- **Suspension**: an admin's block on a person: their sessions end and they cannot sign in until
  it is lifted. What they posted stays.
- **Deletion queue**: the accounts in the 30-day hold between deletion and erasure.
- **Data request**: a person's request for a copy of their data, which must be answered within
  30 days of its receipt.
- **Announcement**: one banner, a notice or a warning, shown at the top of the Popkin app to every
  signed-in person.
- **Limit**: an upload size or a rate limit an Owner can change without a release.
- **Wrap up**: to end a Popkin. A wrapped-up Popkin is read-only and has a recap.
- **Recap**: the summary of a wrapped-up Popkin: its people, messages, photos and top-rated
  moment.
- **Contacts**: a person's own address book. Only its owner sees it.
- **Contact**: one person in someone's Contacts. A contact need not have a Popkin account.
- **Circle**: how a contact is filed, one of Family, Friends, Work, Neighbours and Clubs.
- **Closest**: the few contacts shown first on People.
- **Timeline**: what was logged with a contact: conversations, milestones and memories.
- **Memory**: a photo, a file or a note kept about a contact.
- **Key date**: a birthday, an anniversary or another day that matters to a contact, with its own
  reminder.
- **Follow-up**: something to do for a contact, with a due day. A **promise** is a follow-up the
  person said they would do.
- **Habit**: a rhythm with a contact, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, such as a Sunday call.
- **Group**: a household, small group, team or club of contacts. It can be linked to a Popkin.
- **Connection**: how two contacts, or a contact and the owner, know each other: Family, Partner,
  Friend or Work, with the owner's own words.

## L1-001: Public landing page

**Traces to:** L0-001

A visitor must be able to learn what Popkin is, how it works, what it is used for and that it
carries no ads, and start a Popkin or sign in, from a public page that needs no account.

## L1-002: Accounts without passwords

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to create an account and sign in with only a name and a phone number or an
email address, confirmed by a one-time six-digit code. The system must never ask for or store a
password. A person must be able to change their name and photo, sign out and delete their
account.

## L1-003: Start a Popkin

**Traces to:** L0-001

A signed-in person must be able to start a Popkin in about a minute by giving it a name, an emoji
and a colour and choosing how long it lasts, and must receive its invite the moment it exists.

## L1-004: Invite and join

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to invite people with a QR code or an invite link, without knowing their
phone number. An invited person must be able to see which Popkin they are joining and who invited
them, join it with a name and an optional photo, and be welcomed in.

## L1-005: My Popkins

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to see every Popkin they belong to, active and wrapped up, with what is new
in each, and open any of them, start a new one or scan to join another.

## L1-006: Popkin home

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to see one Popkin at a glance: who is in it, how long it has left, the
latest chat, photos and experiences, and reach chat, photos, experiences, a group call and the
invite from it. The Host must be able to change the Popkin's name, emoji, colour and length.

## L1-007: Chat

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to talk in a thread everyone shares and in direct messages
between two members, with text, photos, emoji reactions and replies, and see new messages without
reloading.

## L1-008: Calls

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to start or join a group video call with one tap, call one
member from a direct message, and control their own microphone and camera.

## L1-009: Photos

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to add photos to a shared album, browse it by day, view a photo
with its caption, react to it, comment on it and save it.

## L1-010: Experiences

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to share experiences (things the group did, restaurants, and
things the group wants to do), rate them from one to five stars, comment on them and see the
group's score.

## L1-011: Wrap up and recap

**Traces to:** L0-001

A Popkin must end when its length runs out or when its Host wraps it up. A wrapped-up Popkin must
become read-only, give every member a recap, and keep or remove its photos as the Host chose.

## L1-012: Privacy and security

**Traces to:** L0-001

A Popkin must be private to its members. The system must authenticate every request, authorise it
against membership and role, validate every input, protect personal data in transit and at rest,
limit abuse and resist the OWASP Top 10. Popkin's own staff see inside a Popkin only to moderate
what its members reported, or for a recorded safety reason (L1-024).

## L1-013: Performance and reliability

**Traces to:** L0-001

The system must respond quickly on a phone over a mobile network, deliver messages in near real
time, and tell people plainly when something is loading, has failed or is offline, without losing
what they typed.

## L1-014: Responsive, accessible and themed

**Traces to:** L0-001

Every screen must work on every viewport from 360 px wide upward and use the width it is given,
with touch on a phone and with the keyboard on a desktop, in a light and a dark theme, and must meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## L1-015: No advertising

**Traces to:** L0-001

Popkin must carry no advertising anywhere in it. Popkin may charge for parts of it one day
(ADR 0019); it must not claim to be free, and until a requirement says what is charged for, no
journey asks for payment.

## L1-016: A Popkin has its own address

**Traces to:** L0-001

Every Popkin must have its own address, `https://{address}.popkin.community`, taken from its name
and settled by its Host when the Popkin is started. The address must belong to that Popkin alone,
open the Popkin for its members, carry its invites, and never by itself let anyone in or show
anything of the Popkin to a person who is not a member.

## L1-017: Plan

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to plan what they are getting together for on a shared plan
board, whatever kind of event it is: break it into stories anyone can add, size and own, move
them from Backlog through To do and Doing to Done, and see on a burn-down whether the plan is on
track and when it will be done.

## L1-018: Schedules

**Traces to:** L0-001

Members of a Popkin must be able to see when their people are free: each member's days off
around the event, week by week, and each member's working hours over the coming week, so the
group can find the day everyone is off and the evening everyone is free, and take the plan to
chat.

## L1-019: Feature switches in the admin app

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must be able to set Plan and Schedules Off, on for chosen Popkins, or on for everyone,
from a separate admin app, without a release, and see who changed a switch and when. A feature that is off must
disappear from the Popkin app and refuse every request, keeping its data for when it returns.

## L1-020: Marketing site

**Traces to:** L0-001

Anyone must be able to learn what Popkin is from a public marketing site, separate from the app,
that shows the pitch film "Meet Popkin" beside what the landing page says, and go from it to the
app to start a Popkin or sign in. The site must need no account, call no API and load nothing
from another host.

## L1-021: Admin overview

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must see at a glance how Popkin is doing: how many people and Popkins there are and how
active they are, what is waiting for an admin, how the platform's services are, and how the daily
figures have moved.

## L1-022: Help a person

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must be able to find a person, see their account, Popkins and sessions, and help them or
protect others from them: suspend them and lift it, sign them out everywhere, start the deletion
of their account or call it off while it is held, and answer their request for a copy of their
data within 30 days.

## L1-023: Help a Popkin

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must be able to find a Popkin by its name or invite code, see its length, members and
activity without its content, and help it: wrap it up now, give it more time, remove a member and
reset its invite.

## L1-024: Moderate reported content

**Traces to:** L0-001

A member must be able to report a message, photo or experience that breaks the rules. An admin
must be able to review each report with enough context to judge it fairly, remove the content or
dismiss the report, and, for a serious case, open one Popkin's shared content for a recorded
reason, while seeing nothing else of any Popkin and no direct message that was not reported.

## L1-025: Keep Popkin running

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must be able to see and end the calls under way, see and run the Worker's jobs, and see
and retry the album exports that have not been delivered.

## L1-026: Announcements

**Traces to:** L0-001

An admin must be able to show one short banner to every signed-in person in the Popkin app, a
notice or a warning, and take it down; a member must see it at the top of the app.

## L1-027: Limits and settings

**Traces to:** L0-001

An Owner must be able to change the upload sizes and rate limits without a release, and any admin
must be able to see, read-only, every setting the deployed system runs with, never the value of a
secret.

## L1-028: Admin roles and the audit log

**Traces to:** L0-001

Each admin must be able to do only what their role allows, give a reason for every change, and
have every change recorded in an audit log that every admin can read and nobody can edit. An
Owner must be able to add and remove admins and change their roles.

## L1-029: Contacts

**Traces to:** L0-001

A signed-in person must be able to keep their own Contacts: the people who matter to them,
whether or not they have a Popkin account. Only its owner sees it, and nobody is told they were
added, imported, connected or deleted. Contacts serves pop-up communities by keeping close the
people a person meets in their Popkins and by starting the next Popkin from the people they keep:
it is reached from My Popkins, imports the people a person shares Popkins with, shows which Popkin
a contact is also in, and opens or starts a Popkin from a group.

## L1-030: Today in Contacts

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to see, in one place, the next date that matters, what is coming up, what
to do today, what they logged recently and who they have not talked to in a while.

## L1-031: People and profiles

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to add, edit, find, filter and delete their contacts, and see each one's
profile: who they are, their key dates, their habit, what the person knows about them, the people
around them and their history.

## L1-032: Timeline and memories

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to log a conversation or a milestone with a contact, with what they
promised, and keep photos, files and notes about them.

## L1-033: Dates and reminders

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to keep their contacts' birthdays, anniversaries and other days, each with
its own reminder, and be reminded in the app, by phone notification and by a weekly email.

## L1-034: Follow-ups

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to keep what they said they would do for their contacts, see what is late,
due today, due this week and later, and mark it done.

## L1-035: Habits

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to keep a rhythm with a contact, see when one is overdue and how often it
has been kept, and log that it was.

## L1-036: Groups and Popkins

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to put contacts in households, small groups, teams and clubs, and from a
group open the Popkin linked to it or start a new Popkin with its people.

## L1-037: Connections

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to record how their contacts know each other and see it as a map.

## L1-038: Import from your Popkins

**Traces to:** L0-001

A person must be able to bring the people they share Popkins with into their Contacts, choosing
who, without those people being told.
