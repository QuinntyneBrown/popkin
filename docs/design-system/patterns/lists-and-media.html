Lists and media

Popkin has no tables. Its lists are cards, rows and photo grids, newest first, loading more as you scroll.

Overview

Popkins are cards, conversations are rows, photos are a grid by day, experiences are picture cards. Lists show the newest first and load the next page as you near the end.

When to use what

ContentUse
PopkinsPopkin cards; wrapped ones as rows
Conversations, membersRows
PhotosPhoto grid by day, with a feature photo
ExperiencesExperience cards with a filter row

States

  • Loading (skeletons), empty, filtered empty, error, offline (saved items with a banner), long (pages of 50 messages or 60 photos).

Responsive behaviour

  • One column of cards in the app column; three photos across.

Accessibility

  • Lists are <ul>; each item is one link.
  • Filters are toggle buttons in a labelled group.
  • New items arriving are announced politely.

Content

  • Lead with the name; one line of latest activity.
