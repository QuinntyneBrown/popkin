Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every press answers within 100ms; waits show the shape of what is coming; outcomes are confirmed where people are looking.

Overview

Choose the lightest feedback that tells people what happened.

When to use what

SituationUse
A screen's first load, longer than 300msSkeletons in its shape
A button's action is in flightBusy button with a present-tense label
Upload with known progressProgress bar with a count
A wait on the dark stageSpinner
Outcome with no visible changeToast (8s, Undo when reversible)
Outcome visible on screenNothing extra
A failure tied to a formAlert in the form
A lasting conditionBanner

Structure

Loading your Popkins…

Skeleton and busy button.

States

  • Loading, loaded, partial (one section failed, the rest work), failed, offline.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons match the layout at each width so nothing moves.

Accessibility

  • Skeletons are hidden; a status line says what is loading.
  • Busy buttons keep a name and cannot be pressed twice.
  • Toasts are announced; errors assertively.

Content

  • Present tense with an ellipsis while busy: "Wrapping up…".
  • Outcome first in toasts: "Experience shared".
