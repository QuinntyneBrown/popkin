Contacts

A member's own address book beside Your Popkins: people, dates, follow-ups, habits, groups and connections, seen by them alone.

Overview

Contacts is a member's own address book beside Your Popkins: the people in their life, the dates that matter to them, what they said they'd do, the rhythms that keep them close, their groups and how everyone knows each other. Only they can see it, and nobody is told when they are added, connected or deleted. It serves Popkin's purpose by sending people back to Popkins: you import contacts from the Popkins you share, a group opens its Popkin or starts one, and a profile says which Popkin you share.

Contacts has no L1 or L2 requirement yet (open question 10 in docs/mocks/README.md); this pattern records what the mocks settle.

Structure

The shell is the Popkin shell (Popkin navigation) with Contacts' seven sections: Today, People, Dates, Follow-ups, Habits, Groups and Connections. The nav's tile is 📇, its line says "13 people · only you", and its exit goes back to Your Popkins. Phones show five tabs; Habits is reached from Today and Connections from Groups and People. Each section is a page header over the Contacts page and grid. Add someone and Log are full screens with a top bar (Cancel · title · Save); everything smaller is a dialog over its page.

States

  • Loading: skeletons in the shape of each section's lists, with a hidden status line ("Loading your dates…").
  • Empty: says what the section is for and offers one action (Add a date, New habit); a new account's Today offers Add someone and Import from your Popkins.
  • Error: keeps the shell and says what is safe ("Your reminders still go out"), with Try again.
  • Offline: what you add is kept on the phone and saved later (contacts-toast/warning).
  • Done: ticking a task or keeping a habit says so in a success toast with Undo.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M: one column, five bottom tabs, Closest and wide lists scroll sideways.
  • From M: the rail with all seven sections; People becomes a table-like list.
  • From L: two columns (.contacts-grid); Groups shows the list beside the open group; the sidebar from L carries the Contacts tile.

Accessibility

  • Every state carried by colour is also in words or shape: late in days, kept by fill, link kinds in a list, kinds of entry by a hidden word.
  • The map is a picture of the list: every link is reachable as text.
  • Message and Call are sms: and tel: links named with the person.
  • Toggle buttons (aria-pressed) for ticks, chips, faces and kinds; tabs as links with aria-current.

Content

  • Write about people as the member would: Mum, Brother, Next door, no. 14.
  • Dates and gaps in days: in 4 days, 2 days late, 59 days ago.
  • Never imply the other person knows: "Only you can see them", "Chidi isn’t told".
  • Use the words the app uses: Popkin, Follow-ups, Habits; never "trip".
