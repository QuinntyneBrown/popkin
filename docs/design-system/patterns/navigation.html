Navigation

Inside a Popkin, four tabs at the bottom. Everywhere else, a top bar with Back. My Popkins is home; the landing page is for visitors.

Overview

The app has three levels: My Popkins, a Popkin (Home, Chat, Photos, Experiences), and things inside it (a thread, a photo, an experience). Each screen's route data sets its chrome and stamps data-page on the body.

When to use what

WhereNavigation
VisitorLanding header and footer; welcome screen
My PopkinsLogo, Scan to join, your avatar; floating New Popkin
Tab roots of a PopkinPopkin tabs
Deeper screensTop bar with Back
StepsTop bar with Back and step dots
Calls and photosStage bar with Minimise or Close

States

  • Current tab: aria-current="page", Popkin colour and weight.
  • Unread: count on Chat.

Responsive behaviour

  • The same at every width; the column centres from 576px.

Accessibility

  • Landmarks: header, main, nav.
  • Back is first in the tab order; it names its destination when needed ("My Popkins").

Content

  • One word per tab.
  • Back is an icon with a name, never the word "Back" alone in a title.
