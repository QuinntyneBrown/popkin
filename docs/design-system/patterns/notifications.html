Notifications

Toasts for outcomes, banners for conditions, counts where people look; outside the app, emails each member chooses, that say why they came. Nothing nags.

Overview

Popkin keeps people up to date with unread counts on My Popkins, the home and the Chat tab, a banner while a call is on or time is running out, and toasts for outcomes.

Outside the app Popkin reaches people by email and on their phone. Each member chooses, per kind, which of the two they get (Notification kinds), how often email comes (as it happens, a daily or a weekly digest), how much each Popkin tells them (Setting list) and whose birthdays to be reminded of. Every email says why it came and offers the narrowest way to stop it first. Admins can pause all member email and hold a single Popkin (Sending status).

States

  • Info, success, warning, danger; stacked toasts newest at the bottom.
  • Settings: email on or off (switches disabled, choices kept), as it happens / daily / weekly, a Popkin on Everything, Just for me or Muted, a birthday reminder on or off, no one muted.
  • Emails: one change or a bundle; a digest with or without "needs you"; a birthday ahead or today; a plan, a to-do or a wrap-up tomorrow.
  • Admin: sending or paused; a kind on or off for everyone; a Popkin held or released; Owner, Support or Viewer.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts above the tabs; banners under the header.
  • Emails are one 600px column at every width.
  • On a phone the settings tabs tighten to fit, a Popkin's level picker goes under its name, and pickers open from the button's start edge.

Accessibility

  • Live regions present from load; errors in role="alert".
  • Counts have meaningful labels.
  • Settings tabs follow the Tabs pattern; level pickers are radio groups; every switch names its kind and channel.
  • The undo bar arrives in a polite live region that is there from load.
  • Pause, Resume, turning a kind off and releasing a hold each confirm in a dialog that traps focus.

Content

  • "Joel popped in", "1 hour left", "Back online. 2 messages sent."
  • An email's footer: "You’re getting this because you’re in Lakeside Weekend and asked for replies by email, as it happens."
  • Narrowest way out first: "Mute Lakeside Weekend · Notification settings · Unsubscribe from all email".
  • The undo bar says what still happens: "You’ll still get a reminder before plans you’re going to."
