Overview

Popkin keeps people up to date with unread counts on My Popkins, the home and the Chat tab, a banner while a call is on or time is running out, and toasts for outcomes.

When to use what

SignalUseLasts
Unread messagesBadge countsUntil read
A call is onPopkin-coloured banner with JoinUntil it ends
One hour leftWarning banner; Host gets ChangeUntil dismissed or changed
OfflineDanger bannerUntil back online
Something workedToast8s, pauses on hover
Something failedToastUntil dismissed

Structure

Group call · 6 on the call
Join
Call banner.

States

  • Info, success, warning, danger; stacked toasts newest at the bottom.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts above the tabs; banners under the header.

Accessibility

  • Live regions present from load; errors in role="alert".
  • Counts have meaningful labels.

Content

  • "Joel popped in", "1 hour left", "Back online. 2 messages sent."
