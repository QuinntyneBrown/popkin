Overview

See Content for the rules and the microcopy library. This pattern shows how the voice shifts with the moment.

When to use what

MomentToneExample
StartingLight, quickPop! Lakeside Weekend is up.
JoiningWelcomingWelcome in, Joel!
EmptyEncouragingIt’s quiet in here. For now.
ErrorCalm, reassuringWe couldn’t send your code. Nothing you typed was lost.
EndingWarm, a little nostalgicThis Popkin wrapped up. What a time.
DestructivePlain, exactDelete your account? This can’t be undone.

Structure

🏕️

Pop! Lakeside Weekend is up.

Hold up this code and let people scan their way in.

A celebratory moment.

States

  • The voice is the same across states; only the tone shifts.

Responsive behaviour

  • Copy is written for 360px: titles fit in two or three lines.

Accessibility

  • Plain words; no idioms that block understanding in errors or destructive confirmations.

Content

  • Cheek in celebrations, never in errors or deletions.
