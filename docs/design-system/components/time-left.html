Component · Identity

Time left

A pill with a quarter-clock and how long a Popkin has: "1 day 6 hrs left", "Until Nov 30", "Here until Sunday, 6 pm".

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole none; text

Overview

Shown on every Popkin with an end date: in lists, on the home header, on invites. A Popkin with no end date shows "Ongoing" instead.

Anatomy

  1. Clock14px ring with a quarter arc.
  2. LabelInter 600 13px in the Popkin colour.
  3. Pill32px, tinted with the colour.

Sizes

One size.

States

1 day 6 hrs left · Wraps up Sun, 6 pm
1 day 6 hrs left · Wraps up Sun, 6 pm

Static display: there is nothing to press, so there are no interaction states. Both themes are shown below.

Responsive behaviour

  • Never wraps; long labels truncate inside a card, and the full time is on the home.

Theming

Text --accent-text on --accent-tint: the colour darkened in light, lightened in dark (see the Popkin colour table in the README).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text with a decorative clock.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The words carry the time; the clock is aria-hidden.

Contrast

Label on the page

Motion

Updated at least once a minute, without animation.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Largest two units: "1 day 6 hrs left", "42 min left".
