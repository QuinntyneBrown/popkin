Status pill

A small pill with a dot and one or two words: what state a record is in, told by its words and its tone.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAdmin app<span>

Overview

Use beside a record's name or in a table column for its state: Active, Suspended, Failed, Running, Queued. Not for counts (use Badge) and not for a message (use Alert).

Anatomy

  1. DotA 6px dot in the text colour; a spinner ring for work under way.
  2. WordsOne or two, bold, 12px.

Variants

ActiveSuspendedBuildingDue in 4 daysQueuedDraft
Tones: ok, bad, busy, warn, neutral, info.
HTML
<div class="cluster"><span class="status status--ok">Active</span><span class="status status--bad">Suspended</span><span class="status status--info status--busy">Building</span><span class="status status--warn">Due in 4 days</span><span class="status">Queued</span><span class="status status--info">Draft</span></div>

Sizes

One size, 24px tall.

States

ActiveSuspendedBuildingDue in 4 daysQueuedDraft
ActiveSuspendedBuildingDue in 4 daysQueuedDraft

Static: a status is read, not pressed. Both themes are shown above; the busy ring stops under reduced motion.

Responsive behaviour

  • Never wraps; in a stacked table row it sits on the right of its line.

Theming

Each tone pairs a semantic background with its foreground: --color-success-*, --color-warning-*, --color-danger-*, --color-info-*.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text in a <span>; the words carry the state, so colour is never the only sign.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The words are the label: Failed, not a red dot alone.

Contrast

Ok
Warn
Bad
Info

Motion

The busy ring spins over --duration-deliberate; under reduced motion it stands still.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • One or two words in sentence case: Active, Being deleted, Due in 4 days.
