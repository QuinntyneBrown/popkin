Choice card

A radio choice drawn as a card: an emoji tile, a title, a sentence of consequence and a radio dot. Used for how long a Popkin lasts.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Radio group

Overview

Use choice cards when each option needs a sentence of explanation. For two short options use a segmented control.

Anatomy

  1. Icon tile44px sand tile with an emoji.
  2. Title and subNunito 800 18px; Inter 14px muted.
  3. Radio24px ring; a dot when chosen.
  4. Card72px minimum, 24px radius, 2px edge; the Popkin colour edge and tint when chosen.

Variants

A group of choice cards.
HTML
<div class="stack stack--sm" role="radiogroup" aria-label="How long it lasts" style="width: 100%;"><button class="option-card" type="button" role="radio" aria-checked="false" ><span class="option-card__icon" aria-hidden="true">☀️</span><span class="grow"><span class="option-card__title">Just today</span><span class="option-card__sub">Wraps up at midnight</span></span><span class="option-card__radio" aria-hidden="true"></span></button><button class="option-card" type="button" role="radio" aria-checked="true" ><span class="option-card__icon" aria-hidden="true">⛺</span><span class="grow"><span class="option-card__title">The weekend</span><span class="option-card__sub">Wraps up Sunday, 6 pm</span></span><span class="option-card__radio" aria-hidden="true"></span></button></div>

Sizes

One size.

States

VariantUnchosenChosenFocus
Card

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards stack and fill the column.

Theming

Chosen: edge and dot --accent-line, fill --accent-tint. The colour follows the Popkin being made.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="radiogroup" with role="radio" buttons and aria-checked; roving tabindex.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the chosen card.
↑ ↓Moves and chooses.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Title and sub are the name; the emoji is aria-hidden.

Contrast

Title on the tint
Sub on the card
Unchosen ring

Motion

Edge and tint fade over --duration-fast.

Touch

72px tall cards; 12px apart.

Content

  • Title says the choice; sub says the consequence in time: "Wraps up Sunday, 6 pm".
