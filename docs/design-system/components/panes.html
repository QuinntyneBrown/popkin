Panes

The columns beside a thread from 1024px: the list pane, the thread pane where the list is the screen, and the detail pane, which is the Popkin or the other member of a direct message.

Overview

Inside a Popkin, a .screen--app grows columns as the screen widens (L2-058 AC11, AC12). Three panes sit beside the screen's own part, each fixed to the viewport and given its width by the screen through --pk-nav-width, --pk-list-width and --pk-detail-width:

  • List pane (.list-pane): the Chat list beside an open thread (.screen--thread), from 1024px.
  • Thread pane (.thread-pane): the Everyone thread beside the Chat list when the list is the screen (.screen--list), from 1024px. Choosing a conversation changes the pane, not the screen.
  • Detail pane (.detail-pane): from 1280px, who is here. Beside Everyone it is the Popkin and the members here now; beside a direct message it is the other member (.detail-pane--person), with "Call {first name}" and the photos shared between the two (.detail-pane__shared).

Anatomy

  1. PaneA named <aside>: "About Walt". Surface background, a hairline on the side of the thread.
  2. AvatarThe other member, extra large (72px). Decoration: the title names them.
  3. Title.detail-pane__title, h2 type.
  4. Place"In 🏕️ Lakeside Weekend", small and muted; the emoji is hidden from assistive technology.
  5. Action"Call Walt", a small secondary button, 20px below the place. Absent when the Popkin has wrapped up.
  6. Label.detail-pane__label, an h3: "Shared between you".
  7. Shared photos.detail-pane__shared: three across, square, 6px apart, 4px radius.

States

The panes have no states of their own; what is in them has. The list pane shows the list's loading skeletons, its error and its compact empty section; the thread pane shows the thread's. The open conversation in the list pane is marked with aria-current="page": the Everyone card deepens its tint and gains an edge, a row turns sand. A wrapped-up Popkin's person pane has no "Call" action.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 1024px no pane is there: display: none takes it out of the tab order and the accessibility tree too (L2-058 AC13). The list is a screen of its own, and the thread is a screen of its own.
  • From 1024px the list pane and the thread pane open; a thread's top bar loses its Back, since the list is beside it.
  • From 1280px the detail pane opens at the end, and the list pane is wider.

Theming

The list pane and the thread pane are on the canvas, the detail pane on the surface; every colour is a role, so both themes follow. The open card takes the Popkin's colour through --accent and --accent-line.

Accessibility

  • Each pane is a landmark with a name: <aside aria-label="Chat">, <section aria-label="Everyone">, <aside aria-label="About Walt">.
  • Headings keep their order: the screen's h1, a pane's h2, its groups' h3.
  • The open conversation says so with aria-current="page", not by colour alone.
  • Nothing a person needs is in a pane only: the list, the thread and the call are each reachable at every width (L2-058 AC13).

Content

  • The person pane's name is "About {first name}"; its action is "Call {first name}".
  • "Shared between you" labels the photos of a direct message; with none, the label is not shown.

Do and don't

Do

Hide a pane with display: none below its width.

Don't

Read the viewport width in code to decide whether a pane is there.

Tokens

--layout-list-pane, --layout-list-pane-xl, --layout-detail-pane, --layout-sidebar-width, --color-bg-canvas, --color-bg-surface, --color-bg-subtle, --color-border-default, --accent, --accent-line, --radius-xs, --space-1-5, --space-5, --space-6, --space-8, --text-h2.

Code

In the app: pk-list-pane, pk-thread-pane and pk-detail-pane (with person) in frontend/projects/components, placed in the list, thread and detail slots of pk-app-screen. The rules are the "Chat" block of the Form factors section of components.css.
