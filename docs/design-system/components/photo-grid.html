Photo grid

The album: square photos three across with 4px gaps, grouped by day; the first photo of the latest day is twice the size.

Overview

Use for the Photos tab. Each photo opens the photo view. The home shows a horizontal strip of the six latest instead.

Anatomy

  1. CellSquare, cropped to fill.
  2. FeatureTwo by two, the first of the latest day.

Sizes

Cells size to the column.

States

VariantDefaultFocusUploading
Cell
Uploading

Responsive behaviour

  • Three across on a phone. In the album, four from 640px, five from 1024px and six from 1280px.
  • From 768px the album sets the grid in from the sides, widens the gaps and rounds the corners of the cells.

Theming

Gaps show --color-bg-canvas; the upload state uses --color-bg-scrim.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A grid of links, each with an <img alt>: the caption, or "Photo by Walt".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOpens the photo.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Alt text is the caption.

Contrast

State text on the scrim

Motion

None.

Touch

Cells are at least 116px at 360px.

Content

  • Day headings: "Saturday", "Friday".
