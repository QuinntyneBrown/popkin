Switch

An on/off setting that takes effect with the action it belongs to. On is the Popkin colour; the line under the label says what on or off means.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="switch"

Overview

Use a switch for one binary setting. The sentence under the title changes with the state so the consequence is always written, not only coloured.

Anatomy

  1. Setting rowWhite card, icon tile, title and consequence.
  2. Track52 × 32px; Popkin colour when on, clay when off.
  3. ThumbWhite 24px circle that slides.

Responsive behaviour

  • The row fills the column; the switch stays at the end.

Theming

Track on: --accent; off: --color-border-strong; thumb: --color-constant-light.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<button role="switch" aria-checked> labelled by the row title and described by the consequence.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Space, EnterToggles.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The title names the setting ("Keep the photo album"), not the action.

Contrast

Off track on the card

Motion

The thumb slides over --duration-base with --ease-pop. Durations come from tokens, which are near zero under prefers-reduced-motion, so the press scale and colour fades disappear without extra CSS.

Touch

The whole row is a label; the switch is 52 × 32px inside a 64px row.

Content

  • Title is the setting; the second line is what it means now.
