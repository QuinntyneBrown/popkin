Contacts page and grid

The body of every Contacts section: its padding, its two columns from L, and the head of each list.

Overview

Every Contacts section sits in the Popkin shell (Popkin navigation with Contacts' seven sections) and lays its body out with these three blocks. .contacts-page is the body's padding, with room under it for the phone's tabs. .contacts-grid splits the body into two columns from L, in one of three proportions. .list-head heads every list with its title and, on the right, the link to all of it. A page heading is the page header; these blocks are what comes under it.

Anatomy

  1. Body.contacts-page: 20px each side, 96px below for the tabs; 32px from M, 48px across from XL.
  2. List head.list-head: the title (Nunito 20px) and its link, 44px tall so the link is a full target.
  3. Count.list-head__count: how many, faint, after the title.
  4. Grid.contacts-grid: one column, 32px apart; two from L, 40px apart.

Layout blocks have no interaction states. The link in a list head is a text button and takes its states. Late is a variant, not a state: it tells you the list holds overdue things, and its title says so in words.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M (768px): body padding 20px, and 96px at the bottom so the last item clears the tab bar.
  • From M: 32px padding; the tabs move to the rail, so the bottom padding drops to 48px.
  • From L (1024px): .contacts-grid becomes two columns. The second column of --about and --aside is the 320px detail pane.
  • From XL (1280px): 48px side padding, and the detail pane grows to the 380px list pane.

Theming

The grid and the body read spacing and layout tokens only. The list head reads --color-fg-accent when late and --color-fg-subtle for the count; both themes keep 4.5:1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Layout. A list head's title is a real heading (h2 on the page, h3 inside a column), so a screen reader can jump from list to list. Sections are labelled by their heading with aria-labelledby.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the list head's link; the grid itself holds no stops.

Focus

The DOM order is the reading order: the lead column comes before the side column, so focus never jumps back up the page.

Labelling

The count is part of the heading's text ("Overdue 1"); the link names its destination ("All dates", not "See all").

Content

  • List titles are short nouns: Coming up, To do today, Recently, This week.
  • The link says where it goes: All dates, All follow-ups.
  • Late is for lists of things already overdue: Overdue. Never for emphasis.
