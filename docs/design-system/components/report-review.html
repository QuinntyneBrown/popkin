Report review

The report queue beside the reported content: a list of reports, the content quoted as posted, who reported it and the author's history, with the decision under it.

Overview

Use only on Reports. The content is shown as it was posted, so an admin judges the thing itself; a removed item keeps its place, marked removed.

Anatomy

  1. Kind and placereport-detail__where: the kind, the Popkin, how long ago.
  2. Reasonreport-detail__title.
  3. Evidenceevidence: the author and the content, text or photo.
  4. FactsReported by, the author's history.
  5. DecisionRemove (danger) or dismiss, and a link to the author's account.

The open report has a bar on its left as well as the fill, and aria-current.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 1024 px the queue sits above the open report; from 1024 px they sit side by side (panel-grid--list-detail).
  • A photo in evidence is 96px square beside text, full width when stacked (evidence--stacked).

Theming

Evidence sits on --color-bg-subtle inside the panel; removed content is --color-fg-subtle and italic.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The queue is a list of links with aria-current on the open one; the detail is a section labelled by its reason.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus through the links and buttons in DOM order.
EnterFollows a link; activates a button (so does Space).

Focus

The shared ring: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

A photo carries alt text describing what was reported; the decision buttons name the kind: Remove message.

Contrast

Evidence text
Removed content
Many reports

Motion

None.

Touch

Queue items are full width; the decision buttons are 36px tall and stack on phones.

Content

  • The reason as the reporter chose it, sentence case.
  • Remove names the kind: Remove message, Remove photo.
