Segmented control

Two or three options in a sand pill; the chosen one is a white raised segment. Phone or email; Match my device, Light or Dark.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Tabs / Radio group

Overview

Use it to switch between two or three alternatives that change the same field or setting. For filtering a list use chips.

Anatomy

  1. TrackSand pill, 4px padding.
  2. Segment36px, Inter 600 14px; the chosen one is white with a soft shadow.

Variants

Tabs: switches the field below (sign up).
HTML
<div class="segmented" role="tablist" aria-label="How to reach you"><button class="segmented__option" type="button" role="tab" aria-selected="true">Phone</button><button class="segmented__option" type="button" role="tab" aria-selected="false">Email</button></div>
Radio group: a setting (theme).
HTML
<div class="segmented" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Theme"><button class="segmented__option" type="button" role="radio" aria-checked="true" aria-selected="true">Match my device</button><button class="segmented__option" type="button" role="radio" aria-checked="false" aria-selected="false">Light</button><button class="segmented__option" type="button" role="radio" aria-checked="false" aria-selected="false">Dark</button></div>

Sizes

One size.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocus
Option

Responsive behaviour

  • Sits beside its label on one row; wraps under it when the row is narrower than both.

Theming

Track --color-bg-subtle; chosen segment --color-bg-surface and --shadow-1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="tablist" with role="tab" and aria-selected when it swaps content (APG Tabs); role="radiogroup" with aria-checked for a setting.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group, onto the chosen option.
← →Moves and selects.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The group has an aria-label; each option is a word.

Contrast

Chosen label
Other labels

Motion

The chosen segment fades over --duration-fast.

Touch

Each option is at least 44px wide and 36px tall inside a 44px track.

Content

  • One word per option.
