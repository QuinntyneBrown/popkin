Form sections

A long form cut into sections, with the parts Add someone needs: a date with its reminder, a chosen person, a grid of kinds.

Overview

Add someone is a long form, so it is cut into sections: Who, Dates to remember, How they connect, Details. From M each section's name and hint sit on the left and its fields on the right. Three parts are made for it: a date entry (a date with how far ahead to hear about it), a person pick (someone chosen, with how they connect and Remove) and the kind grid (what kind of milestone, the chosen one in sun). Fields, chips and switches are the usual text field, chip and switch.

Anatomy

  1. Section.form-section: the name (Nunito 20px) and hint, then the body; ruled off below.
  2. Body.form-section__body: fields 20px apart; __pair two across from 640px.
  3. Date entry.date-entry: what and when, the reminder select, Remove.
  4. Person pick.person-pick: face, name, how they connect, Remove.

Sizes

Kind grid options are 56px tall. The date entry and person pick are as tall as their content, 44px controls inside.

States

PartDefaultHoverChosen

Chosen (aria-pressed="true") fills the option in sun with an ink edge, so it shows without colour too. A field's own states (focus, invalid) are the text field's; an invalid section keeps its place and the form summary links to it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M: each section stacks, its name over its fields.
  • From M: the name and hint in a 160px column, the fields beside it.
  • From 640px: a __pair puts two fields side by side; the date entry puts its parts on one line; the kind grid goes three across.

Theming

Sections read text and border tokens. The chosen kind is --color-warning-loud under --color-constant-dark in both themes. The date entry and person pick are the card, edged in --color-border-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each section is a section labelled by its title. The kind grid is a group of toggle buttons, one pressed at a time (its group is labelled "What kind"). Remove buttons name what they remove.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThrough the fields of each section in order.
Space / EnterChooses a kind; presses Remove.

Focus

Focus moves to the first field of a section when it is reached from the form summary.

Labelling

The date entry's select has a hidden label naming the date ("Remind me about the birthday"). Remove says what: "Remove the connection to Grace".

Motion

None.

Touch

Kind options are 56px tall; Remove is a 44px icon button.

Only one kind can be chosen, so the grid could be a radio group; it is drawn with toggle buttons as in the design. If it moves to the components library, prefer role="radiogroup" with arrow keys (logged in the README).

Content

  • Section names are short nouns: Who, Dates to remember, Details.
  • Hints say why: We’ll nudge you ahead of each one.
  • Optional sections say Optional, once, in the hint.
