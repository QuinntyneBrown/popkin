Banner

A full-width strip under the header for a condition that lasts: a call is on, the Popkin is about to wrap up, you are offline.

Overview

A banner stays while its condition holds and offers at most one action. It is not for one-off outcomes (use a toast).

Anatomy

  1. IconStatus icon.
  2. TextBold lead, then detail.
  3. ActionWhite pill.
  4. DismissOptional.

Banners are not interactive; their action and dismiss are.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of the column, under the header.

Theming

Status pairs --color-*-bg / --color-*-fg; the call banner is the Popkin colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status"; offline uses role="alert" because it changes what works.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOn the action.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The bold lead is the message.

Touch
