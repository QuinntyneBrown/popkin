Keep strip and habit row

A habit's last twelve periods, kept, missed and due; and the habit in a list.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="img"

Overview

A habit is a rhythm with someone: a Sunday call, lunch every two weeks. The keep strip shows its last twelve periods, oldest first: kept is a filled green cell, missed an empty outline, and the one due now a dashed coral outline. Its label says how many were kept. The habit row lists a habit with who, how often, the strip, the streak and when the next one is.

Anatomy

  1. Who.habit-row__who: the face and the habit's title, how often under it; a link to the profile.
  2. Strip.keep-strip: twelve cells, 28px tall, 4px apart.
  3. Kept / missed / due__cell--kept filled green; plain cell an outline; __cell--due dashed coral.
  4. End.habit-row__end: the streak and the next one.

Variants

Due now: the last cell dashed.

Sizes

Default 28px tall (in a spotlight or an info card); --sm 24px (in a habit row). The cells share the width.

States

PartKeptMissedDue
Cell

The cells are not interactive. Fill tells kept from missed, and the dashed edge marks the one due; none relies on colour. The habit row's link takes the focus ring.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M: the habit row stacks: who, then the strip, then the streak and next on one line.
  • From M: three columns, who 1.3 : strip 1 : end auto, the end right-aligned.

Theming

Kept is --color-success-solid; missed is an outline in --color-border-strong; due a dashed --color-danger-border. Every edge is 3:1 or more on the card in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The strip is one image (role="img") whose name says it in words: "Kept 9 of the last 12, the latest one is due". Its cells are not read one by one.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the habit row's link to the profile.

Focus

The focus ring goes round who and the title.

Labelling

The row's link is the title and how often. The streak and next are text.

Contrast

Kept cell on the card
Missed outline on the card
Due outline

Motion

None.

Touch

The row's link is 48px tall at least.

Kept against missed was 2.98:1 when both were filled; now missed is an empty outline, so fill tells them apart and each edge passes on its own.

Content

  • The habit's title says what and who: Lunch with Chidi, Sunday call with Mum.
  • How often in plain words: Every Sunday, Every 2 weeks, First Saturday of the month.
  • The streak as a span: 11 weeks running, 7 months running.
