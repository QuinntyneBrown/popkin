Notification kinds

Every kind of notification with the line that says what triggers it, and a switch for email and one for the phone.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrole="switch"

Overview

Every kind of notification Popkin sends, grouped (When something changes, Reminders, Your account), each with the line that says what triggers it and a switch per channel: Email and Phone. A kind the member can't turn off says "Always on" with a lock instead of switches. The admin console lists the same kinds in a data table with one switch for everyone.

Anatomy

  1. Group headTitle, the group's one-line meaning, and the column names over the switches.
  2. KindName and its trigger in the member's words.
  3. Channel switchesA switch per channel, centred in a 56px column (72px from S).
  4. Lock"Always on" spanning both columns, for sign-in codes.

Responsive behaviour

  • The name and trigger take the rest of the row and wrap; the switch columns never move, so a column reads straight down.
  • Columns are --space-14 wide on a phone and --space-18 from 640px.

Theming

The switches read the Popkin colour of the screen (--accent-line); the rest is neutral ink, muted and subtle text, and hairlines.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list of rows, each with two role="switch" buttons. The column heads are visual only (aria-hidden); each switch carries its full name instead, "New photos by email", so nothing depends on the grid.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Space, EnterToggles the focused switch. It saves at once; an undo bar offers to take it back.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each switch: the kind's name and the channel ("Group chat on your phone"). The group is a section labelled by its title.

Contrast

Trigger line
Column names
Off switch track

Motion

The switches' own slide; nothing else moves.

Touch

Each row is at least 64px tall; each switch is 52 × 32px inside a column at least 56px wide.

Content

  • Name the thing that happens, plural where it repeats: "New photos", "Mentions and replies".
  • The trigger starts with who or what: "Someone new joins a Popkin", "The day before a to-do of yours is due".
  • A required kind says "Always on" to members and "Required" to admins.
