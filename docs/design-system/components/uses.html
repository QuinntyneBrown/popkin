Idea cards

A grid of cards, each one kind of group a Popkin is for: a picture, its emoji and colour, a sentence and how long it lasts.

Overview

Use the grid (uses) and its cards (use) for the ideas on the landing page. A card is not a link and not a Popkin: for a real Popkin in a list, use the card.

Anatomy

  1. Picture4:3, decoration, empty alt.
  2. TileThe idea’s emoji on a tint of its colour (Popkin tile, small).
  3. NameAn h3.
  4. SentenceWhat such a group shares.
  5. LengthHow long such a Popkin lasts, under a little room of its own.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: one column (L2-001 AC5).
  • From 576px: two across.
  • From 992px: four across (L2-001 AC6).
  • Cards in a row are equally tall; the body stretches.

Theming

A card is --color-bg-surface with the first shadow. Its colour comes from data-accent on the card and shows on the tile. The colour is never the only difference between two cards: each has its emoji and its name (L2-057 AC5).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each card is an <article> with an <h3>. The picture and the tile are decoration.

Keyboard

Nothing in it takes focus by itself; the links and buttons it holds are reached with Tab in reading order.

Focus

Nothing takes focus.

Labelling

The h3 names the card.

Content

  • Four ideas: Small group, Hackathon, Team, Family trip (L2-001 AC3).
  • The sentence names real things the group shares. The length is two words: "Keeps going", "One weekend", "One season", "Ten days".
