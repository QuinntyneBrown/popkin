Welcome

The first screen of the app for a visitor: the wordmark, the mark as a picture, the headline, and the three ways in.

Overview

The content of the welcome screen, inside a split screen. It offers Start a Popkin and Join with a code as an action pair, and Sign in as a text button under them. The picture is the Popkin mark with a photograph of a group in its bubble and of one more person in its dot: a group, and someone popping in.

Anatomy

  1. WordmarkLarge, in coral (logo).
  2. PictureThe mark as a picture, 16rem wide, in the middle. Below 768px only.
  3. HeadlineThe screen’s h1, in the display type style.
  4. LeadOne line under the headline.
  5. ActionsThe action pair.
  6. Link"Already have an account? Sign in", as wide as the column.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: one column as tall as the screen. The wordmark is at the top, the picture and the headline share the room that is left, and the actions end the screen, within reach of the thumb.
  • From 640px the two actions are side by side; from 768px stacked again; from 1280px side by side (action pair).
  • From 768px: a split screen. The picture moves to the brand aside and the column’s own is display: none, so it is read once. The column sits in the middle of its part, with 48px between the wordmark and the headline (L2-058 AC10, AC13).

Theming

The screen carries coral (data-accent="coral"): the wordmark, the picture’s fill and ring, and the primary button are --color-accent. The aside is --color-accent-subtle.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The content of the screen’s <main>. The headline is the only <h1>. The picture is role="img" with a name that says what it shows; the wordmark is role="img" named "popkin".

The shared ring.

Labelling

The link reads as one sentence: "Already have an account? Sign in". Only "Sign in" is in the accent colour, and it is bold, so the colour is not the only sign. The wordmark is a logotype, which the contrast criterion exempts; the headline under it says what the screen is.
