Connection map

Who knows whom: your people as faces on a board, joined by lines in the kind of link.

Overview

Connections draws who knows whom: your people as faces on a board, joined by lines, you in the middle. Coral is family, a darker coral a partner, green friends, a dashed grey line work, and a dotted line an introduction you've promised. Choosing a face brings its lines forward, fades the rest, and lists that person's links in words beside the map. The link key is a line's sample, in the filter chips and beside each link in the list.

Anatomy

  1. Board.connection-map: sand, 1000 × 680, 24px inset (40px from M) so faces at the edge are whole.
  2. Lines.connection-map__line--family / partner / friend / work; --pending dotted.
  3. Node.connection-map__node: a face and its first name, a toggle button placed by --x and --y (0–1).
  4. You--you: larger, ringed in coral, not a button.

Sizes

Faces are 28px (you 36px) below M and 40px (you 56px) from M; names grow from 11px to 14px. The board keeps its proportions at every width.

States

PartDefaultPressedDimmed
Face
You
You

Pressed (aria-pressed="true") rings the face in ink and inverts its name; a dimmed face is 35% opaque and still a button. The lines fade in 150ms; reduced motion makes it instant.

Responsive behaviour

  • The board keeps 1000 : 680 and scales; positions are fractions, so faces stay put relative to each other.
  • Below L: the list of the chosen person's links sits under the map; from L beside it, in the detail pane.
  • From M: bigger faces, names and inset.

Theming

Lines read --color-accent, --color-fg-accent, --color-success-solid and --color-fg-subtle. Faces are ringed in --color-bg-surface so they stand off the lines; you in --color-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The board is a group of toggle buttons, one per person, named with the full name (WAI-ARIA Button pattern, aria-pressed). The lines are hidden: what they show is said in words in the list beside the map ("Tomás Reyes — Married") once someone is chosen, and every link can be reached that way.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThrough the faces in reading order, then the list.
Space / EnterChooses a face, or clears it when chosen.

Focus

The focus ring goes round the face and its name.

Labelling

Each face's name is the person's full name; the visible first name is hidden so it is not read twice.

Contrast

Name on its pill
Chosen name
Work line on sand

Motion

Lines and faces fade over --duration-fast; prefers-reduced-motion sets durations to 0.

Touch

Faces are 28px below M, under the 44px target: the board is a picture of the list, and each person is also reachable from People and from the list beside the map. A face's hit area includes its name.

Line colour tells kinds apart, but never alone: work is dashed, pending dotted, the chips name each kind with its key, and the list says the link in words. Coral and green lines are lighter than 3:1 on sand; the words beside the map carry what they show (WCAG 1.4.11 asks contrast only of graphics needed to understand the content).

Content

  • A link in your words: Mum, Married, Old friends, Intro pending.
  • Names on the board are first names; the list uses full names.
  • The empty board says how to start: Connect two people.
