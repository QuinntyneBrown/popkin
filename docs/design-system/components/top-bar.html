Top bar

A 64px bar: back on the left, a title or step dots in the middle, actions on the right. Thread bars add a hairline and stay on top.

Overview

Every screen that is not a tab root has a top bar with Back. Onboarding steps show soft step dots instead of "Step 2 of 3".

Anatomy

  1. Back44px icon button, 28px chevron.
  2. Title or dotsNunito 800 18px with an optional subline; or step dots.
  3. End actionsIcon buttons.

Theming

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header>; step dots are role="img" named "Step 2 of 3".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
—Back is first in the tab order.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Back is named for its destination when it is not obvious ("My Popkins").
