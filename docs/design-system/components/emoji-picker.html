Emoji picker

Twelve emoji in a six-column grid. The chosen one lifts onto white with a ring in the Popkin colour.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableToggle buttons with aria-pressed

Overview

Used once, when naming a Popkin. Twelve curated emoji keep the choice quick; the Popkin's tile uses the result everywhere.

Anatomy

  1. GridSix columns, 8px gap.
  2. Emoji buttonSquare, sand, 26px emoji; chosen: white with a 3px ring.

Variants

The twelve emoji.

Responsive behaviour

  • Six columns at every width; buttons grow with the column and never drop below 44px.

Theming

Ring: --accent-line.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="group" of buttons with aria-pressed; exactly one is pressed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Space, EnterChooses.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each button is named in words ("Camping", "Praying hands"), not by the emoji glyph.

Contrast

Chosen ring on the card

Motion

Press scales over --duration-fast.

Touch

At least 44px square.

Content

  • Emoji names are plain words.
