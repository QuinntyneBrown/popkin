Timeline

How long a Popkin lasts, as a bar from now to its end, with both ends said in words.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDecoration with a text equivalent

Overview

On How long, from 768px, the split aside draws the chosen length as a timeline under the choice's icon and title. The bar fills further the longer the Popkin lasts. Under it, "Now" and the end in words say the same thing, so the bar is never the only carrier.

Anatomy

  1. BarA 12px track on the surface colour, fully rounded, hidden from assistive technology.
  2. FillThe Popkin colour. Its width is set by the modifier, not by a number.
  3. Ends"Now" on the left, the end in words on the right, 14px semibold.

Sizes

One size: 12px tall, up to 30rem wide.

States

NowSunday, 6 pm
NowSunday, 6 pm
NowWhenever you say
NowWhenever you say

The timeline has no interaction states. It changes when another length is chosen.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: not shown, with its aside. Each choice in the form already says when it wraps up.
  • From 768px: as wide as the aside's body, up to 30rem, 40px under the title.

Theming

Track --color-bg-surface on the aside's tint; fill --accent, the Popkin colour from data-accent; ends in the text colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No role. The bar is aria-hidden; the ends are plain text. The fill is not a measurement, so it is not a progressbar or a meter.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The ends read "Now" and the end in words. That, the title above and the chosen option in the form all say the length; colour and width are never the only carriers.

Contrast

Ends on the canvas the tint is mixed into

The bar is decoration, so its fill needs no contrast with the track.

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • The left end is always "Now".
  • The right end is when it wraps up, as the option says it: "Midnight", "Sunday, 6 pm", "Sun, 13 Sep · 6:00 pm".
  • With no end date: "Whenever you say".
