Figures and tiles

Big numbers: figure tabs that choose the chart under them, tiles that link to a list, and a headline figure over a chart.

Overview

Use on the dashboard and metrics for the few numbers an admin checks first. A figure tab changes the chart below it; a tile opens the list it counts. Not for a record's facts (use Key values).

Anatomy

  1. Labelfigure-tab__label.
  2. Valuefigure-tab__value: Nunito black, tabular, 30px then 44px.
  3. Deltafigure-tab__delta: what changed today.
  4. Selected barAn ink bar under the chosen figure, with aria-selected.

The selected figure is ink with a bar under it; the others are muted, so it is told by weight, the bar and aria-selected, not colour alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • Figure tabs stack under 640 px and sit three across from 640 px.
  • Tiles are two across, four across from 1024 px with tiles--4.

Theming

Text tokens and --color-bg-surface; hairlines between tiles show through a 1px gap on --color-border-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Figure tabs: the WAI-ARIA Tabs pattern, each tab aria-controls the chart's tabpanel. Tiles: links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus through the links and buttons in DOM order.
EnterFollows a link; activates a button (so does Space).
← →Move between figure tabs.

Focus

The shared ring: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The label and value together name each figure; the chart's aria-label gives its latest value.

Contrast

Value
Unselected label

Motion

None.

Touch

Whole tiles and tabs are targets, at least 80px tall.

Content

  • Values with thousands separators; deltas say what happened today: +312 joined today.
