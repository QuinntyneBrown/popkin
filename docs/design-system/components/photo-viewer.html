Photo viewer

A photo full screen on the dark stage, with who and when, its caption, reactions and a react bar.

Overview

Opens from the album, the home strip and chat photos. The stage is dark in both themes so photos read the same everywhere.

Anatomy

  1. React barFive quick reactions and More, on a light veil.
  2. ChipsCounts; yours filled white.
  3. CommentsFrom 1024 px, in the side panel; yours with Delete comment.
  4. Comment fieldFrom 1024 px, at the foot of the side panel ("Add a comment", "Post comment").
  5. Photo actions⋯ in the top bar, for whoever may delete the photo.

Sizes

One size.

States

VariantDefaultPressedFocus
Reaction

The stage does not change with the theme; it is dark in both.

Responsive behaviour

  • The photo fits the width without cropping; the info sticks to the bottom.
  • Below 1024 px, Comment and the comment count open the comments dialog, a bottom sheet under 640 px.
  • From 1024 px the info becomes a side panel beside the photo with the author, caption, reactions, the comments and the field "Add a comment" (.photo-info__comments, .photo-info__composer, hidden below with display: none); Comment moves focus to the field (L2-058 AC11).
  • A wrapped-up Popkin shows the reactions and comments with no react bar, no Comment, no field and no Delete comment.

Theming

Stage --color-bg-stage; chips --color-bg-stage-control; pressed chip --color-constant-light on --color-constant-dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page with a heading for the caption; reactions are toggle buttons in a labelled group.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
← →Previous or next photo.
EscCloses.

Focus

The ring on the stage is cream so it reads on dark.

Labelling

Reactions are named in words; pressed ones are marked pressed and outlined. The comment field is labelled "Add a comment"; its send button is "Post comment". The delete button on your comment is "Delete comment", and Photo actions is "Photo actions". The count near the limit is read out politely.

Contrast

Text on the stage
Muted text on the stage

Motion

None; swipes follow the finger.

Touch

44px reaction buttons.

Content

  • The caption is the heading.
  • Comments are text, never HTML: first name, time, then the words.
  • Failures are toasts that stay until dismissed: "We couldn’t save your reaction." and "We couldn’t save this photo." / "Try again in a moment."
