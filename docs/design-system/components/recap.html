Recap

The front page of a wrapped-up Popkin: three photos as prints, who showed up, what they made, the top-rated moment and what comes next.

Overview

The front page of a Popkin that has wrapped up. It shows three of its photos and its emoji, says it is over, counts who showed up and what they made, names the top-rated moment and offers what comes next. Use it once, on the recap screen; a list of wrapped-up Popkins uses the Popkin tile in a row.

Anatomy

  1. Collagepolaroids: three photos as tilted prints, the middle one larger and on top, with the Popkin tile over their lower edge. Decorative.
  2. HeadlineThe h1 and, under it, the Popkin's name and dates.
  3. People and numbersA card: the avatar stack with “14 people showed up”, then a stat pair under a hairline.
  4. Top-rated momentA linked card row: the experience's picture, the label, its title and its rating. Left out when nothing was rated.
  5. ActionsA stack of block buttons: the Popkin-coloured one first, then a secondary, then a quiet one.

Sizes

One size per form factor: the prints are 144 px and 160 px until L, and 224 px and 256 px from L, where the collage is 440 px wide.

States

🏕️
🏕️

The recap itself is static. While it loads, the screen shows skeletons in its shape; when it cannot load, an empty state with Try again. While the album is being prepared, the first action is disabled and a progress bar under it counts the photos.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS: one column, at most 448 px wide: the collage, the headline, the card, the top-rated moment, the actions.
  • S and M: the same column, at most 512 px.
  • L: two columns of equal width, centred in the height of the screen. recap__lead (the collage and the headline) is beside recap__rest (the numbers and the actions); the prints grow and the headline is 36 px.
  • XL: the gap between the columns grows to 96 px.

Theming

The screen behind it is the Popkin's wash (screen--wash). The prints keep a white edge in both themes, as a photograph does; the cards are surfaces. Set data-accent on an ancestor.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page region with one h1. The collage is decorative (aria-hidden, empty alt): the photos are in the album.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThe top-rated moment, then each action, in reading order at every width.

Focus

The linked card and the buttons show the focus ring.

Labelling

The avatars are hidden; “14 people showed up” says it. The stats are a description list.

Contrast

People row
Name and dates

Motion

None.

Touch

The card and the buttons are at least 44 px tall.

Content

  • The headline is “This Popkin wrapped up. What a time.”; under it, the name, a middle dot and the dates (“Fri 11 – Sun 13 Sep”).
  • “14 people showed up”; “1 person showed up” for one.
  • Numbers take thousands separators; their labels are lower case: “messages sent”, “photos shared”.
  • The actions: “Save the album” (left out when there are no photos), then “Pop up another with this crew” for the Host or “Start your own Popkin” for a member, then “Read the chat”.
