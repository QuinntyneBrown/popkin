Toast

An ink card that rises above the tabs to confirm something with no visible change, or to say something failed. Success and info leave after 8 seconds; failures stay.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLive region

Overview

Use a toast for an outcome that has no place on screen. Use a banner for a condition that lasts and an alert for a failure tied to a form.

Anatomy

  1. IconStatus icon in its toast colour.
  2. Title and textInter 700 15px; one line of detail.
  3. ActionUnderlined: Undo, Try again, Say hi.
  4. Dismiss32px button.

Toasts are not interactive as a whole; their action and dismiss buttons have button states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Centred above the tabs, at most 26rem wide; full width minus 16px under that.

Theming

Card --color-bg-inverse (ink in light, cream in dark) with --color-fg-inverse; icons --color-inverse-*-icon.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Toasts render into a role="status" aria-live="polite" region present from page load; failures into role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EscDismisses the latest toast while focus is inside it.

Focus

Toasts never take focus. The timer pauses on hover and focus.

Labelling

The title says what happened.

Contrast

Toast text
Success icon
Danger icon

Motion

Rises over --duration-slow. Durations come from tokens, which are near zero under prefers-reduced-motion, so the press scale and colour fades disappear without extra CSS.

Touch

Action and dismiss are at least 32px, in a card that does not cover the tabs.

Content

  • Title is the outcome: "Experience shared". Text adds one useful detail.
  • Failures say what was kept: "Your stars are back to how they were."
