Photo picker

A 128px avatar with a camera badge. It shows your initial until you add a photo, and says the photo is optional.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <button> + file input

Overview

Used on joining and in your account. The button opens the device's photo picker (<input type="file" accept="image/*">).

Anatomy

  1. Avatar128px; the initial on sand without a photo.
  2. Badge44px circle in the Popkin colour with a camera, ringed with the canvas.

Without and with a photo.

Theming

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <button> that triggers a hidden file input.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Enter, SpaceOpens the photo picker.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

"Add a photo" or "Change your photo"; the line under it says the photo is optional.

Contrast

Initial on sand

Motion

None.

Touch

128px target.

Content

  • "A photo is optional", then "Looking good." once added.
