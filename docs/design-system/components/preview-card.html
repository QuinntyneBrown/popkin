Overview

On Name your Popkin, from 768px, the split aside shows a preview card under the kicker "How it’ll look to everyone". It follows the name, the emoji and the colour as they change. It is a picture of the choices in the form, not a control: everything it shows is already in the form beside it.

Anatomy

  1. CardA raised surface, up to 26.25rem wide, 24px padding.
  2. TileThe emoji on the Popkin colour: a large Popkin tile.
  3. NameWhat has been typed, 22px, on one line. "Your Popkin" until there is a name.
  4. Last messageA sample first message, muted.
  5. FootAn extra small avatar with "Just you, for now", and a time left pill.

The card has no interaction states. It is not a link and takes no focus.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: not shown, with its aside. The name field shows the emoji and colour beside the name instead.
  • From 768px: centred in the aside, full width up to 26.25rem.
  • The name never wraps; it truncates.

Theming

Card --color-bg-surface with --shadow-2, on the aside's tint. The tile and the time left pill take the Popkin colour from data-accent on the screen, so the card changes colour as a colour is picked.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No role. The card is plain text inside the aside named "Preview". It is not a live region: it repeats what the person has just typed or picked.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable. Unlike a Popkin card in My Popkins, it is not a link.

Labelling

The tile and the avatar are aria-hidden; the avatar's image has an empty alt. The name, the message and the foot are text.

Contrast

Name on the card
Last message and foot on the card
Kicker on the canvas the tint is mixed into

Motion

None. The card changes with the form, without a transition.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • Kicker: "How it’ll look to everyone".
  • No name yet: "Your Popkin".
  • The sample message is "You: Welcome, everyone! 👋".
  • The foot says "Just you, for now" and "Just started".
