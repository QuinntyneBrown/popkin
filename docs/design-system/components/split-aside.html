Split aside

The picture half of a split screen. From 768px it takes the left of the screen and shows what the step makes. Below that it is not there at all.

Overview

A .screen--split puts its form in one half and a .split-aside in the other. The aside is a picture of what the step makes: a photograph on sign up and the code screen, the Popkin as others will see it, how long it lasts, the QR code. It repeats nothing a person needs to finish the step, because under 768px it is gone.

Anatomy

  1. AsideA named <aside>, tinted with the Popkin colour or filled by a photograph.
  2. Body32px padding, 56px from 1024px. Centres its content top to bottom.
  3. IconOne emoji, 60px, hidden from assistive technology.
  4. TitleWhat the choice is called, in the display face.
  5. PictureHere a timeline; elsewhere a preview card or a QR card.
  6. TextOne or two sentences on what happens next, muted.

The aside has no interaction states. It is shown from 768px or it is absent.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: display: none. The form is the whole screen.
  • From 768px: fixed to the left edge, full height, 44% wide. The screen is padded left by the same width.
  • From 1024px: 50% wide, 56px padding, larger title and caption; the people list takes two columns.
  • From 1280px: 55% wide.
  • A body taller than the screen scrolls inside the aside.

Theming

Plain --color-bg-subtle; --brand is --color-accent-subtle; --tint mixes 14% of the Popkin colour (--accent) into --color-bg-canvas, so it follows data-accent and the theme. A caption sits on --color-bg-scrim in --color-fg-on-stage, the same in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <aside>, the complementary landmark, named with aria-label: "Popkin", "Preview", "What happens", "Invite code". Below 768px it is display: none, so it leaves the accessibility tree and the tab order with it (L2-058, criteria 10 and 13).

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable. It holds pictures and text, never a control.

Focus

Nothing in it takes focus. Focus stays in the form half.

Labelling

The photograph has an empty alt and the emoji is aria-hidden: both are decoration. The caption, title and text are read in place.

Contrast

The tint is 14% of the Popkin colour over the canvas; the caption is white on the dark veil over a photograph. Neither can be measured from two tokens here, so check them against the screen.

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • Show what the step makes. Say nothing the form does not already say.
  • A caption is one short line and one sentence under it.
  • A kicker is a label in sentence case: "How it’ll look to everyone".
  • Text is one or two sentences about what happens next.
  • Name the aside for what it shows, not for where it is: "Preview", not "Left panel".
