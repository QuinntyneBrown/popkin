Plan

The parts of the Plan tab (L1-017): the sprint chip with its sparkline (pk-sprint-chip, pk-sparkline), the board with its four columns, the story card and its tag dots, the pickers of the story dialogs, the burn-down’s headline and chart, and Biggest open, which the home’s Plan section reuses. Overview

Use these parts only on the Plan tab, the story dialogs, the home’s Plan section and the recap. A Popkin has one sprint, “Planning sprint”, of 28 days from its first story; the sprint holds To do, Doing and Done, and Backlog sits outside it. A story is one job someone can own: a title, points (1, 2, 3, 5 or 8), an owner or no one yet, one of five tags and an optional due date. Colour never carries a state alone: a tag has its name, points have their number and name, a done story has its tick and its column.

States

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640 px each column is 84 % of the width and the board scrolls sideways with snapping (scroll-snap-type: x mandatory); “New story” is a floating action above the tabs (L2-068 AC8).
  • From 640 px each column is 300 px wide (18.75rem) and the board still scrolls sideways.
  • From 1024 px the four columns share the width equally (each at least 14.5rem) and “New story” sits in the header.
  • From 768 px the burn-down chart grows from 240 px to 300 px high and the headline to --font-size-5xl.
  • From 1024 px Biggest open sits beside the chart, 320 px wide (360 px from 1280 px); narrower, under it (L2-071 AC7). On the home it fills its card at every width.
  • The story dialogs are a bottom sheet below 768 px and a panel on the right of the board from 768 px (Dialog, .dialog--panel).

Theming

The sparkline, the chart’s line, forecast and dot, the done tick and a chosen points option take the Popkin’s colour (--accent, --accent-text, set by data-accent on an ancestor). Columns are --color-bg-subtle, cards --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1; Backlog is a dashed --color-border-default edge. The tag dots read --color-tag-*, the same in both themes, and always sit beside the tag’s name. Both themes read every fill from tokens.

Accessibility

Roles

Each column is a <section> named by its column (“To do”) with an <h2>, and its count reads “stories: 4”. A card is one link; its points are named “{n} points” (“1 point”), its owner is role="img" named by first name, and “No one yet” is named the same. The points and the tags are role="radiogroup" with aria-checked; members are toggle buttons with aria-pressed. The chart’s drawing is aria-hidden; the figure is named “Points left by day” and holds a visually hidden paragraph that gives the points left for each day (L2-071 AC5), and the headline and forecast are text. The sparkline is aria-hidden; the chip’s text says the points left and the status.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the views, the sprint chip, “New story” and the cards, column by column.
EnterOpens a story, or the burn-down from the chip.
← →Moves between the points, and between the tags, in a story dialog.
SpaceChooses a member in “Who’s on it?”, or clears them.

Touch

Cards, points, members and tags are at least 44 px tall; the board scrolls sideways under a finger and snaps to a column.

Content

  • Points read “24 pts left”, a column “4 · 11 pts”; a story’s points are named “8 points” or “1 point”.
  • The status reads “7 behind”, “3 ahead” or “on track” (“On track” as the burn-down’s badge).
  • Use “story”, “sprint”, “Backlog”, “To do”, “Doing”, “Done” and “Plan”; never another word for them, and never “trip”.
  • No count of comments until story comments exist.
