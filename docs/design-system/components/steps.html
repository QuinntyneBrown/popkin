Steps

What happens, in order: a numbered list of short steps, each a disc, a heading and a sentence.

Overview

Use it to say how something works in a few steps that happen in order, as "How it works" does on the landing page. For progress through a flow a person is in, use the step dots.

Anatomy

  1. DiscThe step’s number on the brand colour. Decoration: the list is ordered.
  2. HeadingAn h3: what the person does.
  3. TextOne or two sentences, no wider than 24rem.

Variants

  1. 1

    Pop one up

    Give it a name, an emoji and a colour. Pick how long it lasts. That’s the whole setup.

  2. 2

    Share the QR code

    Hold up your phone, or drop the link in a text. Nobody needs your number.

  3. 3

    You’re together

    Chat, share photos, plan things, hop on a call. When it’s done, wrap it up with a recap.

Static.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: one column, 40px between steps (L2-001 AC5).
  • From 768px: three columns, 32px between them.

Theming

The disc is --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent: Popkin’s own coral, not a Popkin’s colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <ol> of <li>, so the order and the count are announced. The number on the disc is aria-hidden, or it would be read twice.

Keyboard

Nothing in it takes focus by itself; the links and buttons it holds are reached with Tab in reading order.

Focus

Nothing takes focus.

Labelling

Each step is named by its h3.

Contrast

Number on the disc
Heading
Text

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • Three steps: "Pop one up", "Share the QR code", "You’re together" (L2-001 AC2).
  • A heading is an action in two to four words. The text says what it takes, plainly.
