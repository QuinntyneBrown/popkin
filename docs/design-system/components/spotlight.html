Spotlight and date tile

The one thing that matters most on a screen, tinted in its colour; and a date drawn as a tile.

Overview

The one thing that matters most on a screen, tinted in its colour: the next date on Today (Grace's birthday, in sun) and the habit that is slipping on Habits (Lunch with Chidi, in coral). There is at most one per screen. A spotlight leads with when, then who, then why, and ends with the one or two things to do about it. The date tile is the date as a square: the weekday or month over the day, filled in sun when the date is two weeks off or less.

Anatomy

  1. When.spotlight__when: Inter 13px semibold, muted; coral with --late.
  2. Who.spotlight__id: the face (64px) beside the title (Nunito 28px, 30px from M) and its subtitle.
  3. Facts.spotlight__facts: two lines with an icon, a rule above; side by side from 640px.
  4. Actions.spotlight__actions: the primary first, at most two.

The spotlight has no interaction states of its own; its buttons take theirs. Late changes the words as well as the colour. Done (We met up) presses the primary button, fills the last period of the strip and says so in a toast.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: the facts stack.
  • From 640px: the two facts sit side by side.
  • From M: the padding grows to 28px and the title to 30px; a --row spotlight puts its actions beside the title.
  • The date tile never changes size.

Theming

The fill is --accent-tint: set data-accent on the spotlight to tint it in the person's or the habit's colour. Dark mixes 22% of the colour into the card instead of 11%. The soon date tile is --color-warning-loud (sun) under --color-constant-dark in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled by its title. The date tile is text: "Sat 19" reads as it is, and the row beside it says "in 4 days" in words.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the actions in order.

Focus

Only the actions take focus.

Labelling

The title is the heading of the section. Each action names its person: Message Grace, Call Chidi.

Contrast

Muted text on a coral tint
A soon date tile
A later date tile

Motion

None.

Touch

Its buttons are 44px tall.

The ratios on a tint are computed apart, as check_contrast.py cannot mix colours: muted text on any tint is 5.46:1 or more, and late coral text on a coral tint 5.05:1 (light) and 5.27:1 (dark). Late coral text on a sun tint would be 4.48:1 in dark, so --late is used on coral only.

Content

  • When first, in words and days: "Saturday · in 4 days", "Two days overdue".
  • Facts that help you act: what they're into, what happened last time.
  • Actions that name the person: Message Grace, not Message.
