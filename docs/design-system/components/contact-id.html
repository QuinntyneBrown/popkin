Contact header and facts

The head of a profile: face, name, how you know them, Message · Call · Log; and the facts, likes and memories beside the history.

Overview

The head of a person's profile and the details beside their history. Contact header: the face, the name, how you know them, a Popkin you share, and Message · Call · Log. Facts: an icon and a fact per line. Likes: what they love, as soft pills. Memory grid: their photos with a caption each.

Anatomy

  1. Face96px with their colour.
  2. Name.contact-id__name: Nunito 900, 30px, 36px from M.
  3. Sub and alsoHow you know them · circle · city; then a Popkin you share, as a link.
  4. Actions.contact-id__actions: three equal buttons on a phone, right-aligned from M.

Static; the actions are buttons and take their states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M: the header stacks, face and name, then the three actions as a full-width row of equal thirds.
  • From 640px: the actions get their full padding.
  • From M: the name and the actions share a row, the actions at the end.
  • Memory grid: two across, four from 640px.

Theming

Likes read --accent-tint: set data-accent on the list. Facts' icons read --color-fg-subtle.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The name is the page's h1. Facts are a description list: each dt holds the icon and a hidden name (Phone), each dd the value. Likes are a list. Each memory is a figure whose image's alt is its caption.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the also link, Message, Call and Log.

Focus

Only the also link and the actions take focus.

Labelling

Message and Call are sms: and tel: links; their visible words are the names.

Contrast

Name
Also
Fact icon

Motion

None.

Touch

The actions are 44px tall.

Each fact names itself for a screen reader, since the icon alone carries the kind on screen; the mocks indented the value under the browser's dd margin, now reset.

Content

  • Sub: how you know them · circle · city, in that order.
  • Also names the Popkin in quotes: Also in your Popkin “Lakeside Weekend”.
  • Likes are nouns, as they'd say them: Man City, Vinyl — Fela, Sade.
