Closing band

The last word of the landing page: a heading and a paragraph on Popkin’s coral, with the main action in white beside them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLanding page only

Overview

Use it once, as the last section before the footer, to repeat the main action. On the landing page it carries the promise that Popkin has no ads (L2-061 AC3).

Anatomy

  1. BandA rounded block of the brand colour inside the container.
  2. HeadingThe section’s h2, 40px.
  3. ParagraphOne or two sentences, no wider than 30rem.
  4. SideThe white button and a short note under it.

Variants

No ads. Like, actually none.

No ads, no tracking, no feed tuned to keep you scrolling. Just a place for your people, for as long as you need it.

Start a PopkinNo app store detour. Works in the browser.
The one closing band.
HTML
<section class="container section" aria-labelledby="no-ads-h">
  <div class="cta-band">
    <div>
      <h2 id="no-ads-h">No ads. Like, actually none.</h2>
      <p>No ads, no tracking, no feed tuned to keep you scrolling…</p>
    </div>
    <div class="cta-band__side">
      <a class="btn btn--on-accent" href="/sign-up">Start a Popkin</a>
      <span>No app store detour. Works in the browser.</span>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: one column, 24px of padding at the sides.
  • From 576px: 48px at the sides.
  • From 992px: two columns, the action at the end of the line.

Theming

The band is --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent in both themes. The button is the "on colour" one: --color-constant-light with --color-constant-dark text, which does not change with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A block inside a <section> that is named by the band’s h2.

Content

  • Heading: "No ads. Like, actually none."
  • Say what Popkin does not do: no ads, no tracking, no feed. Never say Popkin is free (ADR 0019).
  • The action is "Start a Popkin", the same words as everywhere else.
