Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill the column; experience cards keep 16:9 pictures.
  • On the landing page idea cards go 1, 2 then 4 across.

Theming

Surface --color-bg-surface; tint --accent-tint; shadow --shadow-1, darker in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A whole-card <a> with a heading inside; or a plain <div> when it is not a link.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOpens it.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only. The ring follows the card's radius.

Labelling

The link's name is its heading and text; badges carry their own labels.

Contrast

Text on a card
Muted text on a card

Motion

Press scales to 97%. Durations come from tokens, which are near zero under prefers-reduced-motion, so the press scale and colour fades disappear without extra CSS.

Touch

Whole card is the target.

Content

  • Lead with the name; one line of latest activity.
