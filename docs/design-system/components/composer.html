Composer

The message bar at the foot of a thread: Add, a pill field with emoji and photo, and a coral Send. A reply bar sits above it when replying, and the photo preview, or why a photo was refused, when a photo is chosen.

Overview

One composer per thread. It sticks to the bottom; the field keeps a draft while you are away.

Anatomy

  1. Add44px sand button.
  2. Field48px pill with emoji and photo inside.
  3. Send44px coral; disabled until there is text, a photo or both.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sticks to the bottom of the column with 24px for the home indicator.
  • The photo preview is 112px wide at 4:3 at every width; the refusal alert fills the composer's width and wraps.

Theming

Bar --color-bg-canvas; field --color-bg-surface with --color-border-strong; reply bar --color-bg-surface-sunken with a coral rule; photo preview --radius-md with --shadow-1, and Remove photo an icon-btn--card; refusal alert --color-danger-bg with --color-danger-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form>; the field is labelled by its placeholder text in aria-label ("Message everyone").

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterSends.
Shift+EnterNew line.
EscCancels a reply.
Enter on PhotoOpens the device's picker: one photo, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC (a phone's picker offers the camera). No dialog.
Enter on Remove photoTakes the photo away; focus returns to Photo.

Focus

The field gets the coral edge and halo; Say hi to everyone moves focus here.

Labelling

Every button is named: Add, Emoji, Photo, Send, Cancel reply, Remove photo, Dismiss. The preview's image has the alt text "Photo to send". The refusal is a role="alert", so it is announced when it appears.

Contrast

Field edge
Placeholder
Reply bar text

Motion

None.

Touch

Every control is 40px or more inside a 48px bar.

Content

  • Placeholders name the audience: "Message everyone", "Message Walt".
  • A refused photo is explained in the album's words: "This file is 14 MB. Photos can be up to 10 MB." or "This isn’t a JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photo, so it won’t be added."
