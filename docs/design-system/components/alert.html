Alert

A rounded box inside a screen or dialog that says an action failed and what was kept, or warns before something happens.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="alert"

Overview

Use an alert at the top of the content when a submission failed for a reason that is not a field. Field problems use inline errors and the form summary.

Anatomy

  1. IconStatus icon.
  2. TitleBold: what happened.
  3. TextWhat to do; what was kept.
