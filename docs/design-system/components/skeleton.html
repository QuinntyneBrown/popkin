Skeleton

Sand shapes in the layout of the content that is loading, with a slow shimmer. A hidden status line tells assistive technology what is loading.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablearia-hidden with a status line

Overview

Use skeletons for a screen's first load after 300ms. Use the button's busy state for actions, and the spinner only on the dark stage.

Anatomy

  1. ShapesText, title, circle, tile, card, media, bubble, button.
  2. ShimmerSand to line and back, 1.4s.

Variants

A Popkin card loading: the size of the card it stands in for.

Media, bubble, button.
HTML
<div class="stack stack--sm" style="width: 100%;" aria-hidden="true"><span class="skeleton skeleton--media"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--bubble w-60"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--btn w-full"></span></div>

Sizes

Shapes take the size of what they stand in for.

States

One state: loading.

Responsive behaviour

  • Shapes match the real content at every width so nothing moves when it arrives.

Theming

--color-bg-subtle to --color-bg-subtle-hover.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Shapes are aria-hidden; a visually hidden role="status" says "Loading your Popkins…".

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The status line names what is loading.

Contrast

Shape vs page (decorative)

Motion

The shimmer stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • "Loading your Popkins…", not "Loading…".
