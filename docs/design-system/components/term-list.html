Term list

A short list in a dialog where each item starts with a bold term and goes on in a sentence: what is removed and what is kept when an account is deleted.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableA plain <ul>

Overview

Two to four facts a person must read before a final step, each led by the word that sorts it. It sits in a dialog's body between an alert and a field. It is not a description list: each item reads as one sentence.

Anatomy

  1. ListA ul spaced as a small stack, in small text.
  2. TermBold, ending in a colon.
  3. SentenceThe rest of the item.

Variants

  • Removed: your name, photo, phone number and direct messages.
  • Kept, without your name: your messages and photos in Popkins.
Delete your account: what is removed and what is kept.
HTML
<ul class="stack stack--sm text-sm"><li><strong>Removed:</strong> your name, photo, phone number and direct messages.</li><li><strong>Kept, without your name:</strong> your messages and photos in Popkins.</li></ul>

Sizes

One size: small text, 12px between items.

States

  • Removed: your name, photo, phone number and direct messages.
  • Kept, without your name: your messages and photos in Popkins.
  • Removed: your name, photo, phone number and direct messages.
  • Kept, without your name: your messages and photos in Popkins.

Static: it has no interactive state.

Responsive behaviour

  • Same at every width; items wrap within the dialog.

Theming

Text in --color-fg-default; nothing else is coloured.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <ul> of <li>; the term is <strong>, so the list's count and each item's emphasis are read.

Keyboard

Nothing to focus.

Contrast

Text

Content

  • A term of one to four words, then a colon.
  • A sentence fragment that ends with a full stop.
