Component · Media

Film

The pitch film of the marketing site: a still with a play button and the film’s length, then the film with the browser’s own controls, and its chapters under it.

Overview

Use it once, in “See Popkin in action.” on the marketing site (L2-080). Nothing plays or downloads before the visitor asks: the still is a picture, and the film has preload="none". The film’s narration is burned into the picture, so the film needs no caption track (L2-080 AC5).

Anatomy

  1. Frame: 16:9, rounded, on the constant dark.
  2. Still: a frame of the film, hidden from assistive technology.
  3. Play button: the whole still is the button, named “Play the Popkin film”; the round coral mark is its picture.
  4. Length: the film’s length, “1:38”.
  5. Chapters: a list named “Chapters”, each a time in the accent text colour and a title.

Variants

One. Without chapters the list is left out.

Sizes

One size: the width of its container.

States

Before play

Playing from a chapter

The still gives way to the film with its controls; the chapter chosen is aria-current="true", tinted and bold.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: a 64px play mark and the chapters one under another.
  • From 576px: a 96px play mark and the three chapters side by side.

Theming

The frame and the length’s pill are constant, --color-constant-dark and --color-constant-light in both themes, as a film is. The play mark is --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent. A chapter’s time is --color-fg-accent; the current chapter is on --color-bg-subtle.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A button over a video named by aria-label; an ol of buttons named “Chapters”. The current chapter carries aria-current="true", so it is told apart by more than its colour.

Keyboard

KeyDoes
Enter / Space on playPlays from the start and moves focus to the film, whose own controls then answer the keyboard.
TabFrom the film to its controls, then the chapters.
Enter / Space on a chapterPlays from that chapter’s time and marks it current.

Motion

The play mark grows slightly on hover; with reduced motion it does not.

Content

  • The play button: “Play the Popkin film”. The film: “Meet Popkin”.
  • Times as the film shows them, “0:16”; titles in sentence case, without a full stop.

Tokens

PartToken
Frame--color-constant-dark, --radius-xl, --shadow-2
Play mark--color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent, --space-16 / --space-24
Length--color-constant-light, --text-meta
Chapter--color-fg-accent, --color-bg-subtle, --target-comfortable
