Email

Every Popkin email: one 600px column with one headline and one button, and a footer that says why it came and how to stop it, narrowest first.

Overview

How every Popkin email is drawn. The mock shows the envelope a mail client gives it (subject, sender, preview), then one 600px column: the wordmark and the Popkin it is about, one headline, what happened, one main button. The footer always says why it arrived and offers the narrowest way to stop it first (this Popkin, this person), then all settings, then unsubscribing from everything.

The parts below are the content an email carries: a reply, photos, plan changes, what else is new, a digest's "needs you" list and its Popkins, a birthday, a plan with who's going.

Email clients ignore most CSS. The product's email templates inline these values and use tables for layout; these classes are the reference the templates match, and the mocks' rendering of them.

Anatomy

  1. EnvelopeSubject, sender "Walt via Popkin", time and preview: drawn in the mock only; the mail client draws its own.
  2. CardWhite, 600px, wordmark and the Popkin chip.
  3. HeadlineWhat happened, in display black.
  4. ContentThe reply, photos, changes or list.
  5. Main buttonThe Popkin colour, or coral.
  6. FooterWhy it came; mute this, settings, unsubscribe; the address.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column at every width; nothing sits side by side below 600px, so Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail draw it the same.
  • Below 640px the card's padding drops from 36 to 24px and the envelope gains 12px either side.
  • Photo grids keep three columns; a digest's strip keeps four squares.

Theming

The main button and the reply's tint take the Popkin's colour (data-accent); emails about no one Popkin (the digest, a birthday) use coral, or the contact's colour. In the mocks, dark theme draws the email on the app's dark surfaces. A real mail client chooses its own dark rendering, so the templates set their colours explicitly and keep text on the white card.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An email is a document: the headline is the email's top heading, sections have headings ("Also new in the last 10 minutes"), images have alt text where they carry content and empty alt where they repeat it. Tables used for layout carry role="presentation".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterFollows a link.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Every link says where it goes ("Mute Lakeside Weekend", "See the album"); none says "click here". The preview text is the first line of what happened.

Touch

The main button is 52px tall; footer links are separated by a dot and wrap onto their own lines on a phone.

Content

  • Subject: who did what, where ("Walt replied to you in Lakeside Weekend"); a reminder leads with when ("Tomorrow 8:30: …").
  • Sender: the person "via Popkin" when one person caused it; "Popkin" otherwise.
  • One headline, one main button that names its destination, at most one quieter second action.
  • The footer's first sentence says why this person got this email.
