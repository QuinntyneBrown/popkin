Page header

The title of an admin page with its subtitle, an optional way back and the page's actions on the right.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAdmin app<div> + <h1>

Overview

Use once at the top of every admin page. A detail page adds a back link to its list and a lead (an avatar or a Popkin tile) beside the title. Not inside a panel; a panel has its own title.

Anatomy

  1. Back linkpage-head__back: chevron and the list's name, alone on its line.
  2. LeadAn avatar or Popkin tile beside the title, on detail pages.
  3. TitleThe page's single <h1>, Nunito black.
  4. Subtitlepage-head__sub: what the page shows, or the record's key facts.
  5. Actionspage-head__actions: buttons and status, wrapping under the title on phones.

Only the back link and the actions are interactive; the actions follow the button's own states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Title and actions share a row and wrap: under 768 px the actions drop under the title, left-aligned.
  • The back link is always alone on its line above the title.

Theming

Text tokens on the sand page; the back link's hover fills with --color-bg-surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The page's only <h1>; the back link is an ordinary link, not a button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus through the links and buttons in DOM order.
EnterFollows a link; activates a button (so does Space).

Focus

The shared ring: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The back link names the list it returns to (People, Popkins), never just Back.

None.

Touch

The back link is 32px tall with padding on both sides.

Content

  • Titles are the section's name or the record's name: Dashboard, Mara Okafor.
  • The subtitle says what is current: Mon 14 Jul, 10:42 · figures refresh every minute.
