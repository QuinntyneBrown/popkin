Tabs

Views of one page side by side: words over an accent underline in the app, a segmented pill with counts in the admin.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrole="tablist"

Overview

Tabs switch between views of one page without leaving it: the member's notification settings (what you hear about, Popkins, People) and the admin console's Kinds, Holds and Defaults. The app draws them as words over an accent underline; the admin draws them as a segmented pill with counts, the same control as its filters.

Use tabs when the views are siblings of one setting and someone moves between them. Use the segmented control to choose a value, and the Popkin tabs (Popkin navigation) to move between a Popkin's sections.

Anatomy

  1. Tab listA row with a hairline under it; scrolls sideways, never wraps.
  2. TabDisplay type, extrabold; muted until chosen.
  3. Underline3px in the app accent under the chosen tab.
  4. PanelThe role="tabpanel" after the list, 24px below it.

Responsive behaviour

  • The list scrolls sideways when the tabs are wider than the column; it never wraps to a second line.
  • Below 640px each tab loses 4px of padding each side and steps down to --font-size-md-minus, so "What you hear about · Popkins · People" fits 312px.
  • The segmented variant scrolls inside its pill on a phone, as the admin toolbar's filters do.

Theming

Muted: --color-fg-subtle; chosen: --color-fg-default with a --color-accent underline; the hairline is --color-border-default. The segmented variant reads the segmented control's tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA Tabs pattern: role="tablist" with a label, role="tab" with aria-selected and aria-controls, and one role="tabpanel" labelled by the chosen tab. Only the chosen tab is in the tab order (tabindex="-1" on the rest).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
← / →Moves to the previous or next tab and shows it (automatic activation: the panels are already loaded).
Home / EndFirst or last tab.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The tab list is named for the page section ("Notification settings"); each tab is a word or two.

Contrast

Unselected tab on the page
Chosen tab
Underline against the page

Motion

The underline moves with the chosen tab; nothing animates beyond the colour fade.

Touch

Each tab is 44px tall (--control-height-md); the row scrolls with a finger.

Content

  • Name the view, not an action: "Popkins", "Holds", not "Manage Popkins".
  • A count goes beside a name only when it is the list's length ("Holds 4").
