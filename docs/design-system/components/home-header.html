Component · Layout

Home header

The head of the Popkin home, on a wash of the Popkin colour: the Popkin, a few of its people, how long it has, and its actions. With it, the two-column layout of the home and the head of each of its sections.

Overview

Use it once, at the top of the Popkin home, in a .screen--app above a flush body. It answers three questions at a glance: which Popkin this is, who is in it, and how long it has. Its two actions, Group call and Invite, are the ones a member reaches for first. Under it the .home layout holds the sections of the home, each under a .section-head. Every other tab of a Popkin has a top bar or a tab header instead.

Anatomy

  1. BarBelow 768px only: back to My Popkins at the start, Popkin settings at the end. Two icon buttons.
  2. TileThe large Popkin tile, 64px, hidden from assistive technology.
  3. NameThe screen's h1, on one line, truncated.
  4. MembersUp to four 28px avatars, stacked, "+N" for the rest, and the count in words.
  5. Time leftThe time left pill, 16px under the name.
  6. ActionsGroup call in the Popkin colour, Invite as the secondary button; from 768px Popkin settings on a white disc beside them.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: the bar leads back to My Popkins and holds Popkin settings. The actions are two equal buttons across the header.
  • From 768px: the bar is display: none, because the rail leads back. The actions are a row and Popkin settings (.home-header__more) joins them. The name is larger.
  • From 1024px: the body is a grid; the actions move beside the name and the time left, aligned to the bottom.
  • From 1280px: 40px of padding at the sides and a larger name.
  • .home: one column of sections below 768px. From 768px the sections of .home__side sit two abreast under .home__main. From 1024px .home__main and .home__side are two columns, three to two, and the side sections stack again.
  • A wider home shows more of the same things, never different ones: a fourth message, a fifth and sixth member (.only-l-up), a second and third experience (.only-m-up), six or eight photos.

Theming

The background mixes 10% of the Popkin colour (--accent) into --color-bg-canvas, so it follows data-accent and the theme. The avatars' rings are --color-bg-canvas. Group call is .btn--popkin: the Popkin colour with --color-fg-on-popkin. Popkin settings from 768px is .icon-btn--card: --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1, so it reads as a control on the wash.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> that holds the screen's only h1, the name of the Popkin. Each section of the home starts with an h2 in its .section-head.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through My Popkins, Popkin settings, Group call and Invite, in the order they are read. Controls hidden at a width are display: none and leave the tab order.
EnterFollows a link or presses a button.

Focus

The standard focus ring on every control. Invite opens a dialog and focus returns to it on close.

Labelling

The tile and the avatars are aria-hidden; the name and "14 members" say what they show. The icon buttons are named by aria-label. Every "See all" link ends in visually hidden words that say what it leads to: "See all photos". While loading, the h1 is a visually hidden "Popkin home" and a role="status" line says "Loading this Popkin…".

Contrast

Name on the canvas the wash is mixed into
Member count on the canvas the wash is mixed into
Section count on the page

Motion

None of its own. The skeleton's shimmer stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Every control is at least 44px. The two actions are 48px tall and half the header wide on a phone.

Content

  • The name is the Popkin's own, on one line; a long name is truncated, never wrapped.
  • Count people in words: "14 members", "1 member".
  • Show at most four avatars and "+N" for the rest. One member is one avatar, without a stack.
  • The time left reads its two largest units and when it wraps up: "1 day 6 hrs left · Wraps up Sun, 6 pm".
  • Two actions, named by what they do: "Group call", "Invite".
  • A section head is one or two words, a count where there is one ("48 photos", "4 shared", "14"), and "See all" where there is more.
