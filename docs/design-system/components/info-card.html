Info card and add card

A sand card of facts, and the dashed card that starts what is missing.

Overview

The info card is a sand card of facts: a habit on a profile (how often, its title, the strip, when it is due) or a group's place and note. The add card is its empty counterpart, a dashed card that starts the thing that is missing: Make a habit of seeing Femi, Add a photo.

Anatomy

  1. Over.info-card__over: an icon and the kind, muted.
  2. Title.info-card__title: Nunito 20px; a link when it leads somewhere.
  3. BodyA keep strip or .info-card__lines, an icon beside each.
  4. Foot.info-card__foot: what's next; --late in coral.

The info card is static; a linked title takes the link's states (underline on hover). The add card washes in sand on hover.

Responsive behaviour

  • It fills its column at every width: the profile's details pane from L, the full width below.

Theming

The fill is --color-bg-subtle; lines and over read --color-fg-muted. The add card's edge is a dashed --color-border-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A habit card is a section whose heading is its title. The add card is a link (or a button when it opens a dialog in place).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the linked title, or the add card.
EnterFollows it.

Focus

The ring goes round the add card; the title takes the link's ring.

Labelling

The add card's text names what it starts and for whom. Icons are hidden.

Contrast

Text on sand
Muted on sand

Add card edge

Motion

None.

Touch

The add card is 44px tall at least.

A habit's title reads in its own colour, not as a link colour: the mocks drew it underlined in coral, which read as late. The underline now shows on hover only.

Content

  • Over: the kind or how often, short.
  • Foot: what's next or what's late, with the date.
  • An add card starts with a verb and names the person: Make a habit of seeing Femi.
