Task

A follow-up or a promise: tick it, read it, see who and when. With the field that adds one.

Overview

A follow-up: something you said you'd do, or meant to. You tick it, read it, and see who it is for and when it is due. A promise says so, with where you made it. Ticked, it is struck through and stays until you leave the screen, so you can take it back. The add field above a list adds one in a line; a promise is the one a conversation made, shown in the log before it is saved.

Anatomy

  1. Tick.task__check: a 44px button round a 28px ring; filled green with a tick when done.
  2. Text.task__text: Inter 16px semibold.
  3. Meta.task__meta: who (a 20px face and first name, a link) and, for a promise, where you made it.
  4. Due.task__due: right-aligned; --late in coral.

Done is aria-pressed="true" on the tick: the ring fills green, the tick shows, the text is struck through and fades, and the tick's name becomes "Mark not done: …". Late is said in words ("2 days late") as well as coral.

Responsive behaviour

  • The due date stays on the right at every width; the text wraps.
  • The meta wraps under the text when it runs out of room.
  • On Today the card variant fills the side column; from L it sits beside the lead.

Theming

The done fill is --color-success-solid with the tick in --color-fg-on-success: white on the light theme's green, black on the dark theme's lighter green. The empty ring is --color-border-strong, 3:1 or more on the card and on sand.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The tick is a toggle button (aria-pressed), WAI-ARIA Button pattern. The task is a list item; who is a link to the profile.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabTick, then who.
Space / EnterTicks or unticks.

Focus

The ring sits inside a 44px button, and the focus ring goes round the button.

Labelling

The tick's name repeats the task: "Mark done: Return Ruth’s ladder". The add field has a hidden label, "Add a follow-up"; the placeholder is an example, not the label.

Contrast

Empty ring on the card
Empty ring on sand
Tick on the done fill
Late on sand

Motion

None; the strike-through is instant.

Touch

The tick is 44px square; who is 32px tall with the text beside it.

Undo: ticking shows a success toast with Undo (contacts-toast/success).

Content

  • Start with a verb: Return Ruth’s ladder, Introduce Dev to Priya.
  • Due as a day or as late: Today, Thu, Sep 17, 2 days late.
  • A promise says where you made it: You promised · Chat on Sep 3.
