Colour picker

The seven Popkin colours as 48px circles. The chosen one has a white dot and a double ring.

Overview

The colour a Popkin picks tints its screens, buttons, bubbles and badges for everyone in it. See Popkin colour.

Anatomy

  1. Swatch48px circle in the colour.
  2. ChosenWhite dot, then a canvas gap and a ring in the colour.

Variants

Coral, Teal, Sky, Berry, Leaf, Plum, Amber.
HTML
<div class="swatches" role="group" aria-label="Colour"><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="coral" aria-label="Coral" aria-pressed="false"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="teal" aria-label="Teal" aria-pressed="true"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="sky" aria-label="Sky" aria-pressed="false"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="berry" aria-label="Berry" aria-pressed="false"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="leaf" aria-label="Leaf" aria-pressed="false"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="plum" aria-label="Plum" aria-pressed="false"></button><button class="swatch" type="button" data-accent="amber" aria-label="Amber" aria-pressed="false"></button></div>

Sizes

One size.

States

VariantDefaultChosenFocus
Swatch

Responsive behaviour

  • Wraps to a second row on narrow screens (five then two at 360px).

Theming

Each swatch sets its own data-accent; the ring reads --accent-line.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="group" of buttons with aria-pressed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Space, EnterChooses.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each swatch is named ("Berry"); the chosen one is pressed and carries a dot, so colour is not the only cue.

Contrast

White dot on the colour
Plum swatch on the page

Motion

Press scales over --duration-fast.

Touch

48px circles, 12px apart.

Content

  • Colour names are single words.
