Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: logo and Start a Popkin only; Sign in moves to the footer.
  • From 576px Sign in shows; from 768px How it works and Ideas too.

Theming

Text tokens and the footer hairline.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> with <nav aria-label="Site">; a <footer> with <nav aria-label="Footer">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterFollows; section links move focus to the section heading.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.
