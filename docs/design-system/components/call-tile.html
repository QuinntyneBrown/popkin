Call tile and controls

A rounded tile per person on the black stage with their name, a mic-off icon when muted, and a ring in the Popkin colour when speaking. Under it, Mute, Camera and Leave.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableList of tiles; toggle buttons

Overview

Used on group calls and one-to-one calls. Two people: one column; three or more: two columns.

Anatomy

  1. Video or avatarVideo fills the tile; with the camera off, the avatar.
  2. Name labelOn a dark veil, with a mic-off icon when muted.
  3. Speaking ring3px, the Popkin colour lightened toward white.

Variants

  • Walt, speaking
  • SOMuted, Sam
  • RLRuth

Sizes

Tiles size to the grid.

States

Mute
Camera off
Leave

A pressed control is white with an ink icon and changes its label ("Unmute", "Camera off"). The stage is black in both themes.

Responsive behaviour

  • Tiles fill a two-column grid at every width; at most 12 show at once.

Theming

Tile --color-bg-stage-tile; name label --color-bg-stage-label; controls --color-bg-stage-control; Leave --color-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Tiles are a labelled list; controls are buttons with aria-pressed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Space, EnterToggles a control or leaves.

Focus

Cream ring on the stage.

Labelling

Tiles carry the name plus hidden "Muted" and "speaking"; controls are named for their effect.

Contrast

Name on the tile
Control label
Leave icon

Motion

None.

Touch

64px controls; Leave is 80 × 64px.

Content

  • "You" for your own tile.
