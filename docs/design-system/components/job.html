Job row

A background job: its name, what it does, its schedule, its last run and status, with its details and log under it when open.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAdmin app<tr> + <button aria-expanded>

Overview

Use only on Jobs, one row per Worker job. Opening a row shows its last runs and the log of the failed one.

Anatomy

  1. Togglejob__toggle: the name and what it does; it opens the details.
  2. FactsSchedule, last run, how long it took, next run.
  3. StatusOk, Failed or Running.
  4. Run nowAn action, disabled for a Viewer.

The toggle is a button with aria-expanded; it takes the focus ring like any button.

Responsive behaviour

  • A data table: stacked records under 768 px, the details full width under the row.

Theming

The log sits on --color-bg-subtle in mono.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A disclosure (WAI-ARIA Disclosure pattern): the name is a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls on the details row.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus through the links and buttons in DOM order.
Enter, SpaceOpens or closes the details.

Focus

The shared ring: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The toggle's text is the job's name.

Contrast

Log
Error

Motion

None.

Touch

The whole name is the target.

Content

  • Jobs are named for what they do: Prepare album exports.
