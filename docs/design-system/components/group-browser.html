Group browser

Your groups by kind, and the one you open beside them: who, where, what's coming up, and its Popkin.

Overview

Groups lists your households, small groups, teams and clubs by kind; choosing one opens it beside the list from L, or in the list's place on a phone. An open group shows who is in it, its place and note, what is coming up, and the way into its Popkin: Open “Thursday Night Group” when it has one, Start a Popkin with them when it hasn't. This is where Contacts meets Popkins.

Anatomy

  1. List.group-browser__list: the groups under their kind, 400px wide from L, ruled off.
  2. Item.group-browser__item: the group's tile, name and members; aria-current when open.
  3. Detail.group-browser__detail: its tile, kind, name and actions, then its parts.
  4. Back.group-browser__back: Groups, on a phone only.

Current (aria-current="true") fills the item in sand with an ink edge on its left, from L, where the list and the open group show together. Below L the open group replaces the list, so there is nothing to mark.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below L: one or the other. The list, or (--detail) the open group with a Groups link back; the page's heading stays above.
  • From L: the list (400px) beside the open group, a rule between; the back link hides.
  • From XL: the group's people beside its place, note and dates.

Theming

Tiles take the group's colour. The current item reads --color-bg-subtle with a --color-fg-default edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The list is a nav labelled "Your groups"; the open item has aria-current="true". The open group is a section labelled with its name.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThrough the groups, then into the open group's actions.
EnterOpens a group.

Focus

Opening a group on a phone moves focus to its name.

Labelling

Each item's name is the group's name and its members. Kinds are headings, so a screen reader can jump between them.

Content

  • Kinds are plurals: Households, Small groups, Teams, Clubs.
  • Members by first name, in the order you added them.
  • The way in names the Popkin: Open “Thursday Night Group”. Without one: Start a Popkin with them.
