Trend chart and uptime

A daily series as a solid line over a light area, and thirty days of uptime as bars.

Overview

Use the trend for one series over days; uptime for a service's last thirty days. Not for comparing many series; the admin app draws one line per chart.

Anatomy

  1. GridThree dashed lines at a quarter, half and three quarters.
  2. AreaThe line's colour at 8%.
  3. Line2.5px, round joins.
  4. TodayA ringed dot on the last point: today is partial.
  5. LabelsThe first, last and every fourth day.

Sizes

Fills its container's width; the dashboard draws it 220 units high.

States

Static. Both themes are shown above.

Responsive behaviour

  • The chart scales with its panel; labels thin out to the first, last and every fourth day so they never collide at 360 px.

Theming

Reads currentColor: ink by default, --color-danger-fg with trend--bad. Uptime bars read the success, warning and danger solids.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<svg role="img"> with an aria-label naming the series and its latest value; uptime is aria-hidden because its percentage is written beside it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The label says the series and the latest value: People active each day, latest 9,610.

Content

  • Every chart has a title above it saying what it counts and over how long: Last 14 days.
