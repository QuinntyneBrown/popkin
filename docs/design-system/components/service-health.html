Service health

A summary of how the platform is, then a card per service with its figure, its note and thirty days of uptime.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAdmin app<section>

Overview

Use only on Health. The summary says in one line whether anything is wrong and since when; each service says its own state in words.

Anatomy

  1. Summaryhealth-summary: a title and a line, tinted by the worst state.
  2. Serviceservice: name, status, a figure, a note.
  3. UptimeThirty bars and the percentage.

Variants
