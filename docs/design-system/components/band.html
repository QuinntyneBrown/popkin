Band and section

One section of the landing page: a band across the page, its content in the page’s container under the section’s heading.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLanding page only

Overview

The landing page is a column of sections. Each is a <section> named by its h2, with generous room above and below. Some sit on the canvas; a band sets one apart with a surface of its own, across the full width of the page, while the content stays in the container. Inside the app, a screen’s sections use the section head instead.

Anatomy

  1. SectionA <section>, named by its heading.
  2. ContainerKeeps the content no wider than 72rem (L2-058 AC5); container--narrow is 56rem.
  3. HeadingThe section’s h2. A place the navigation jumps to has an id and tabindex="-1".
  4. LeadOptional. Under the heading, or beside it at the end of the line in a section__head.

Variants

For every group that forms around a table.

Weekly, weekend-long or one-off. Each Popkin gets its own colour and emoji.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 992px: 80px above and below the content. From 992px: 96px.
  • The container’s margin is 20px, and 32px from 576px.
  • In a section__head the lead sits at the end of the heading’s line where both fit, and drops under the heading where they do not.

Theming

A band is --color-bg-surface between two hairlines; a sand band is --color-bg-subtle with no lines. Both follow the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<section aria-labelledby> pointing at its h2, so each section is a named region. The headings are the page’s outline: one h1 in the hero, an h2 per section, h3 inside.

Keyboard

Nothing in it takes focus by itself; the links and buttons it holds are reached with Tab in reading order.

Focus

When the navigation jumps to a section ("How it works", "Ideas"), the page scrolls to it and moves focus to its heading, which is why the heading has tabindex="-1" (L2-002 AC5). It is not a tab stop.

Labelling

The heading names the section. A section whose content brings the heading (the closing band) points at that one.

Motion

Scrolling to a section is smooth unless reduced motion is asked for.

Touch

Nothing to press.

Content

  • A heading is a short sentence with its full stop: "Scan. Join. You’re in.", "Better than a group chat."
  • A lead is one or two sentences.
  • Alternate canvas and band so that two bands never touch.
