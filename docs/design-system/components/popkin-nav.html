Popkin tabs

Four tabs at the bottom of a Popkin: Home, Chat, Photos, Experiences. The current one is in the Popkin colour with a heavier icon and label; Chat carries the unread count.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<nav> with aria-current

Overview

Shown on the four tab roots of a Popkin. Screens deeper than a tab (a thread, a photo) hide it and show a top bar with Back.

Anatomy

  1. TabIcon over a 12px label, 56px tall.
  2. CurrentPopkin colour, bold, 2.5px stroke.
  3. CountCoral badge on Chat.

Variants

  • Home
  • Chat
  • Photos
  • Experiences

Tabs are links; hover and focus follow links. Current is aria-current="page".

Responsive behaviour

  • Fixed to the bottom of the column at every width, with 20px for the home indicator.

Theming

Bar --color-bg-surface; current --accent-text; others --color-fg-muted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav aria-label="Popkin"> of links; the current one has aria-current="page".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOpens the tab.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each tab has a visible label; the badge says "3 unread messages".

Contrast

Tab label

Motion

None.

Touch

Each tab is a quarter of the width and 56px tall.

Content

  • One word per tab.
