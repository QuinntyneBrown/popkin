Progress and spinner

A Popkin-coloured bar for uploads with a count under it, and a white spinner for waits on the dark stage (joining a call, loading a photo).

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="progressbar"; role="status"

Overview

Use the bar when progress is known. The spinner is only for the stage, where skeletons would not make sense.

Anatomy

  1. TrackSand, 8px, rounded.
  2. FillThe Popkin colour.
  3. Label"Uploading 2 of 3".

Variants

Uploading 2 of 3

Determinate bar.
HTML
<div style="width: 100%;"><div class="progress progress--62" role="progressbar" aria-label="Uploading" aria-valuenow="62" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><span></span></div><p class="text-caption" style="margin-top: var(--space-1);">Uploading 2 of 3</p></div>
Spinner on the stage.
HTML
<span class="spinner" role="status" aria-label="Joining the call"></span>

Sizes

One size each.

States

Uploading 2 of 3

Uploading 2 of 3

One state: in progress.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its container.

Theming

Track --color-bg-subtle; fill --accent; spinner --color-fg-on-stage.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with value, minimum and maximum; the spinner is role="status" with a label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; it is read in place.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

Labelled with what is in progress.

Contrast

Label

Motion

The spinner rotates even with reduced motion (it is a status), but slower durations are honoured.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Count, not percent: "Uploading 2 of 3".
