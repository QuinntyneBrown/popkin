Dialog and sheet

A panel over the dimmed, inert page. Under 640px it is a bottom sheet with a grip; from 640px a centred card. Used for invites, forms, confirmations and the settings menu.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Dialog (Modal)

Overview

Use a dialog for a short task that keeps the person in place: invite, edit, comment, confirm. Longer flows are pages.

Anatomy

  1. GripOn sheets only.
  2. HeaderTitle (Nunito 900 22px) and Close.
  3. DescriptionOne line.
  4. Body and footerContent, then actions stacked full width.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sheet): a sheet from the bottom, full width, 32px top corners, grip shown.
  • From 640px: centred, 28rem wide, all corners rounded, no grip.
  • The body scrolls inside 92% of the viewport height.

Theming

Panel --color-bg-surface-raised (the paper colour, raised over the scrim); scrim --color-bg-backdrop; shadow --shadow-3.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="dialog" aria-modal="true" labelled by its title and described by its line; the page behind is inert. Built on the Angular CDK Dialog.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabCycles inside the dialog.
EscCloses and returns focus to what opened it.

Focus

Focus starts on the first field or the safe action ("Stay", "Copy invite link"); never on the destructive one.

Labelling

The title is the name.

Motion

The sheet slides up and the card pops in over --duration-slow; the scrim fades. Durations come from tokens, which are near zero under prefers-reduced-motion, so the press scale and colour fades disappear without extra CSS.

Touch

Close is 44px; buttons are full width.

Content

  • Titles are questions for confirmations ("Leave Lakeside Weekend?") and short noun phrases otherwise.
  • Name the consequence in the description.
