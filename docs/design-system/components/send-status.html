Sending status

Whether member email is going out at all, with the figures, and Pause or Resume behind a confirmation.

Overview

The one control that matters in an incident: whether member email is going out at all. It sits above the admin's Notifications tabs, says so in a word and a figure, and offers Pause or Resume, each behind a confirmation.

Anatomy

  1. DotSuccess green while sending, danger while paused; the title says the same in words.
  2. Title"Email is sending" or "All member email is paused".
  3. FiguresSent today, bounces and speed; or what is queued, since when, by whom and why.
  4. ActionPause (secondary) or Resume (primary).

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px the action sits under the text at full width; from 768px it sits at the end.

Theming

Sending: --color-bg-surface, --color-success-solid dot. Paused: --color-danger-bg, --color-danger-border, --color-danger-solid dot, text in --color-fg-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section headed by its state. When paused it is a role="status" region, so a change of state is announced. The dot is decoration; the title carries the state, so colour is never the only signal.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOpens the Pause or Resume confirmation.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

A disabled action keeps a title saying who can do it ("Support admins can’t do this").

Content

  • Paused says how much is waiting, since when, who paused it and their reason, and what still goes out.
  • Figures are today's, with units: "48,210 sent today · 0.4% bounced".
