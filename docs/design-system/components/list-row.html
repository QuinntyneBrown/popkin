List row

A 72px row: avatar or tile, a title, one line of text and a time or action. Used for direct messages, members, wrapped-up Popkins and menus.

Overview

Use rows for lists of people or threads. Unread rows are bold with an unread dot.

Anatomy

  1. LeadAvatar or tile.
  2. Title and textInter 600 16px; Inter 15px muted, one line each, truncated.
  3. MetaTime or action at the end.
  4. DividerHairline under the text only.

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows fill the column; text truncates, the time never does.

Theming

Divider --color-border-default; hover --color-bg-subtle at 60%.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<ul> of <a> rows; non-interactive rows are <li>. A menu row is an <a> when it goes somewhere and a <button> when it acts where it is; a destructive row says so in its words, never by its colour alone.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
EnterOpens.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The row's text is its name; the unread dot is named "Unread".
