Activity lists

Short lists of things that happened or are waiting: needs attention, recent admin actions, a record's Popkins and sessions, an announcement's history.

Overview

Use for five to ten items inside a panel, each one line or two. attention links to where the thing waits; log says who did what; records lists a record's related items; history times past changes. For longer lists use Data table.

Anatomy

  1. DotWaiting (warning) or urgent (danger); urgent is also said in hidden text.
  2. TitleWhat is waiting.
  3. DetailWhere and how old, one line, truncated.
  4. ChevronIt opens a list.

    Linked items fill on hover and take the focus ring; log lines are read, not pressed.

    Responsive behaviour

    • Single column at every width; a detail line truncates with an ellipsis rather than wrapping.

    Theming

    Text tokens; dots read --color-warning-solid and --color-danger-solid.

    Accessibility

    Role and pattern

    Lists of links (attention, records) or of text (log, history).

    Keyboard

    KeyAction
    Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus through the links and buttons in DOM order.
    EnterFollows a link; activates a button (so does Space).

    Focus

    The shared ring: --focus-ring-width of --color-focus-ring at --focus-ring-offset, on :focus-visible only.

    Labelling

    An urgent item adds "(urgent)" in visually hidden text, so the dot is not the only sign.

    Contrast

    Title
    Detail
    Waiting dot
    Urgent dot

    Motion

    None.

    Touch

    Linked items are full width and at least 56px tall.

    Content

    • Attention titles count and name: 5 reports to review. Log lines read as a sentence: Aiko removed content Message in Class of 2015 Reunion.
    • Times are the clock for today, the date before.
