Rating

Stars in sun yellow: a read-only score with its number, a five-star input for your own rating, and the group's score card with a bar per star.

Overview

Ratings belong to experiences. The number is always shown with the stars, so the score never depends on the yellow.

Anatomy

  1. AverageNunito 900 44px, one decimal.
  2. StarsFilled to match; decorative.
  3. BarsOne per star value, sun on sand.

Theming

Stars --color-star; empty --color-star-empty; bars track --color-bg-subtle.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Read-only stars are role="img" with "4.8 out of 5". The input is a role="radiogroup" of five role="radio" buttons.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the group.
← →One star less or more.
SpaceChooses.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each star button is "1 star" to "5 stars"; the score card lists each bar's count as hidden text.
