Upload row

One photo about to be added: its thumbnail, its caption field, and what happened to it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableA list item with a labelled field

Overview

The Add photos dialog lists one row for each photo chosen. A row shows the photo small, takes its caption, and says what happened to it: refused before the upload, uploading, added, or not uploaded, with a way to try again. The rows sit in a list, a progress bar counts them while they upload, and an alert above them sums up a failure.

Anatomy

  1. Thumbnail64px, the photo cropped square. Its state lies over it.
  2. Caption fieldA pill field, 48px high, with a label for assistive technology.
  3. ReasonWhy the row was refused or failed, with an icon.
  4. Try againOnly on a row that did not upload.

Responsive behaviour

  • The thumbnail keeps its size; the field takes the rest of the row at every width.
  • The reason wraps under the field.

Theming

The field is the text field’s; the state over the thumbnail is --color-fg-on-stage on --color-bg-scrim in both themes; the reason is --color-danger-fg; Try again is --color-fg-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list item. The field has its own label, “Caption for photo 2”, hidden from sight because the thumbnail shows which photo it is. The thumbnail’s text alternative is “Photo 2”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the caption field, then to Try again when the row has it.
Enter / SpaceOn Try again, uploads the photo again.

Focus

The field shows the text field’s focus edge; Try again shows the focus ring.

Labelling

A refused or failed row marks its field aria-invalid and describes it with the reason. The state over the thumbnail is text (“Uploading”), or a tick with the hidden word “Added”; colour never carries it alone.

Contrast

Reason
Try again

Motion

None.

Touch

The field is 48px high. Try again is a line of text with a 44px target around it.

Content

  • The label counts from one: “Caption for photo 1”.
  • The reason says what is wrong and what is allowed: “This file is 14 MB. Photos can be up to 10 MB.”
  • A failure is short and offers the way on: “Didn’t upload.” and “Try again”.
