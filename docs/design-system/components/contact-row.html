Contact row

A person in the People list: who, how you know them, and how long since you talked; Closest above it.

Overview

A person in the People list. On a phone it is their face, their name and how you know them, with when you last talked on the right. From M the row is a table row in all but markup: the circle, the next date and when you last talked line up under a header. Someone gone quiet for more than 30 days says so in coral, in days. Above the list, Closest is a strip of the starred people's faces, and the circle chips filter the list with a count each.

Anatomy

  1. Who.contact-row__who: the 48px face, the name (semibold) and how you know them (muted).
  2. Cells.contact-row__cell: circle, next date. Hidden below M.
  3. Last talked.contact-row__last: right-aligned; --quiet after 30 days.
  4. Header.contact-list__head: the columns' names, from M; hidden from assistive technology.

The row is one link to the profile: it washes in sand on hover and takes the focus ring all round. There is no selected state; the profile opens in its place.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below M: two columns, who and last talked. The circle and the next date are hidden; the profile has them.
  • From M: four columns, 2 : 1 : 1.3 : 1, under the header. Long dates truncate with an ellipsis.
  • Closest scrolls sideways below M, bleeding to the screen's edges; from M it sits in the column.

Theming

The row reads text tokens only; hover mixes 60% of --color-bg-subtle into the page. Quiet is --color-fg-accent. Faces take the person's colour through data-accent on the avatar.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list of links (ul > li > a). Not a table: each row is one action, and its cells are read as one link's name. The header is aria-hidden, so each value carries its own words: the last cell starts with a hidden "Last talked".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the next link in it.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The focus ring goes round the whole row.

Labelling

The link's name is the row's text: "Sam Ortega, Former manager, Work, Birthday Mar 11, Last talked 59 days ago". Closest's links are the first name under the face; the face is hidden.

Touch

Each row is 72px tall at least; each Closest face 80px wide with its name.

Gone quiet is said in words ("59 days ago" rather than a day), not by the coral alone.

Content

  • How you know them, in your words: Brother, Former manager, Next door, no. 14.
  • Last talked as a day this week (Sun, Thu), a date this year (Sep 5), or days ago once it is past 30.
  • The next date as the date and what it is: Oct 7 · Birthday.
