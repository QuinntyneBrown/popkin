Schedule

The parts of the Schedule tab (L1-018): the header with its two views, the weeks of a month, the time-off table, and for this week the day picker, the free headline and the shifts timeline; the rows of “My hours”; and the home’s Schedule card.

Overview

Use these parts only on the Schedule tab and the home’s Schedule section. Time off shows a week at a time, a row per member and a column per day; this week shows seven evenings from today and who works when. Each member changes only their own row, in a dialog. Colour never carries a state alone: every cell, block and count also says it in words.

Sizes

One size.

States

StateWhat it shows
LoadingSkeleton rows in the table’s and the timeline’s shape, and a status that says “Loading the time off…” or “Loading the hours…”.
EmptyEvery weekday Working and “Nobody has added time off yet.”; this week says “Nobody has added their hours yet.”.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640 px the time-off table scrolls sideways inside the screen, its member column fixed; the page does not scroll sideways (L2-072 AC9).
  • From 1024 px the free headline and its action sit side by side (L2-074 AC10).
  • The day picker’s seven days share the width; the shifts’ names keep a fixed column and the track takes the rest.

Theming

Off and the event’s days take the Popkin’s colour (--accent-*, set by data-accent on an ancestor); Tentative is a dashed edge; Working is --color-bg-subtle; blocks are cards (--color-bg-surface, --shadow-1). Both themes read every fill from tokens.

Accessibility

Roles

The time-off table is a real <table> with a caption, scope="col" days and scope="row" members; a weekend cell is role="img" named “Weekend”. The day picker is a list of links with aria-pressed. The shifts figure is named “Who is working on {weekday}” and holds a visually hidden paragraph that reads every member’s stretches (L2-074 AC6); its drawing is aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the views, the weeks, the days and the actions in reading order.
EnterChooses a week or a day.
← →Moves between Off, Tentative and Working in a day of “Add my time off”.

Labelling

Each hours field is named “{Weekday} start” or “{Weekday} second end”; its error is its description.

Touch

Every chip, day and field is at least 44 px tall.

Content

  • Counts read “3 of 6 off” and “4/6 free”; the count is bold when every member but at most one is off.
  • Times read “7:30 pm”, “Midnight”; blocks read “Working till 5:30 pm”.
  • Use “time off”, “this week” and “working hours”; never another word for them.
