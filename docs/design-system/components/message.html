Message

Chat bubbles: theirs on white with a name and avatar, yours on the Popkin colour. With replies, photos, reactions, day dividers and system notes.

Overview

One bubble per message. Consecutive messages from one person show the name and avatar once. Your own messages are on the right with their time.

Anatomy

  1. Avatar and name32px avatar, first name in Inter 600 13px.
  2. Bubble24px radius with a 6px tail corner, max 78% wide.
  3. Reactions28px chips overlapping the bubble's foot.

Responsive behaviour

  • Bubbles are at most 78% of the thread; photos 16rem wide.
  • The thread fills the column at every width.

Theming

Theirs: --color-bg-surface. Yours: --accent with --color-fg-on-popkin. Your reaction: --color-accent-subtle with a 2px coral edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The thread is a <main> region labelled "Messages"; new messages are announced politely with their sender. Reactions are buttons with aria-pressed. Press-and-hold, or focus and Enter, opens the message actions.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Enter on a messageOpens reactions, Reply, Copy.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

Each bubble has its sender (visually hidden for consecutive ones and yours); reactions say the emoji's name, the count and whether you reacted.

Their text

Time

Motion

New messages appear without animation; reaction chips change instantly.

Touch

Reaction chips are 28px with 4px gaps, beside a 44px press-and-hold target (the bubble).

Content

  • System notes are warm and short: "Joel popped in. Say hi! 👋".
  • Day dividers: "Today", "Yesterday", then the weekday.
