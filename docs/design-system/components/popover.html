Picker popover

A small card under its button with a search and a short list to pick one from.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrole="dialog"

Overview

A small card that opens under the button that called it, with a search on top and a short list to pick from: Mute someone in the member's settings, Hold a Popkin in the admin. Picking closes it and adds the choice to the list on the page.

Anything with more than one field, or a consequence to confirm, is a dialog instead.

Anatomy

  1. Add buttonDashed pill with a plus; aria-expanded while open.
  2. CardRaised surface, 19rem wide at most, 8px under the button.
  3. SearchA sand field that filters as you type.
  4. OptionsFace or tile, name, and a meta line (relationship, code).

Variants

Opens to the right, under a button at the start of a row.

Sizes

One size: as wide as its content up to --layout-popover-max, never wider than 85% of the window.

States

VariantClosedHoverFocusOpenDisabled
Add button

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px every popover opens from the button's start edge, since the button may have wrapped to the start of its row; popover--end only takes effect from 640px.
  • Its width caps at 85vw so it never leaves the screen.

Theming

--color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-2 and a hairline, so it reads as above the page in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A non-modal role="dialog" labelled by its button's words, opened by a button with aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded. Focus moves to the search on open and returns to the button on close. Built on the CDK Overlay.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the control in DOM order.
Enter / SpaceOn the button, opens; on an option, picks it and closes.
EscCloses and returns focus to the button.
↓From the search, moves into the options.

Focus

The shared ring: 3px --color-focus-ring (ink in light, cream in dark) at a 2px offset, on :focus-visible only.

Labelling

The search has a visually hidden label; each option's name is its accessible name, with the meta line after it.

Motion

Pops in over --duration-base with --ease-pop; near zero under reduced motion.

Touch

Options are 44px tall; a tap outside closes it.

Content

  • The button says what you will do: "Mute someone", "Hold a Popkin".
  • The search says what it searches: "Search your contacts", "Name or code".
