Foundation

Shape

Everything is round: full pills for actions, 16 to 32px corners for surfaces, a squircle for Popkin tiles.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark
  • Overview

Overview

Five radii plus full and the tile squircle. Radius grows with the element: 16px fields, 24px cards and bubbles, 32px sheets.

Radii

--radius-xs
--radius-sm
--radius-md
--radius-lg
--radius-xl
--radius-full
--radius-tile

Usage

  • Buttons, chips, pills, avatars: full.
  • Fields, thumbnails, alerts: md (16px).
  • Cards, bubbles, choice cards: lg (24px); a bubble's tail corner xs (6px).
  • Sheets, dialogs, the hero card: xl (32px).
  • Popkin tile and app icon: the 32% squircle.
  • Borders: hairline 1px for dividers, 2px for field edges, 3px rings.
