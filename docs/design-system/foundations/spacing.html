Foundation

Spacing

A 4px scale. Related things sit 8 to 12px apart; screen sides are 24px; sections breathe at 28 to 32px.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark
  • Overview
  • Scale
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Every margin, padding and gap is a --space-* token; the number is the multiple of 4px (--space-1-5 is 6px). Larger steps (up to --space-55) size art such as the welcome circle.

Usage

  • Inside a component: 4 to 16px (--space-1 to --space-4).
  • Screen sides: 24px (--space-6); lists and tab screens 16 to 20px.
  • Between a heading and its content: 8 to 12px; between sections: 28 to 32px.
  • Chips and avatars in a row: 8px; block buttons stacked: 12px.
