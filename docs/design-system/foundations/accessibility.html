Foundation

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes, with touch on a phone and the keyboard on a desktop. Colour is never the only carrier of a state.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark

Overview

These are the system's commitments. Each component page shows how it meets them: role and pattern, keyboard, focus, labelling, contrast, motion and touch.

Perceivable

  • Text 4.5:1, large text and UI edges 3:1, checked for 144 pairs in both themes plus every Popkin colour mix.
  • Status is colour plus icon plus words; unread is bold plus a dot; chosen is a ring plus a dot.
  • All sizes in rem; screens reflow at 320px.
  • Photos carry their caption as alt text; decorative SVGs are hidden.

Operable

  • Everything works with Tab, Enter, Space, arrows and Escape.
  • One focus ring: 3px ink (cream in dark) at a 2px offset.
  • Targets 44 × 44px.
  • Toasts last 8 seconds and pause on hover and focus; errors stay.
  • Every page starts with a skip link; one h1 per screen.

Understandable

  • Visible labels; placeholders are examples only.
  • Errors name the field and the fix, sit under the field, and a summary links to them.
  • Destructive actions are confirmed and name the consequence.
  • No passwords: sign in with a six-digit code, paste allowed, one-time-code autofill.

Robust

  • Native elements first; ARIA patterns from the APG where needed (dialog, radio group, tabs, switch).
  • Status messages in live regions.

Testing

  • check_contrast.py for every pair in both themes.
  • check_design_system.py for labels, names, alt text, landmarks and links.
  • By hand: tab through each page; a screen reader on a dialog, a form and the chat; 200% zoom; reduced motion.
