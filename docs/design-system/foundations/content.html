Overview

Popkin talks like a friend organising the weekend.

  • Your people, popped up in seconds.
  • Scan. Join. You’re in.
  • It’s quiet in here. For now.
  • Every good weekend ends.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere; "popkin" lowercase only in the wordmark, "Popkin" in sentences.
  • Buttons are verbs: "Pop it up", "Join", "Say hi to everyone". Never "OK", "Submit", "Click here".
  • The way out is warm: "Not yet", "Stay", "Keep my account".
  • Use the Popkin's name: "Go to Lakeside Weekend", "Wrap up Lakeside Weekend?"
  • Address one person: "you".

Numbers, dates and times

  • Time left in two units: "1 day 6 hrs left", "42 min left".
  • Relative in lists ("9:41", "Fri"), absolute in detail ("Saturday, 7:02 am").
  • Thousands separators: "1,286 messages sent".
  • Counts with nouns: "14 members", "48 photos from 11 people".

Errors

What happened, that nothing was lost, what to do: "We couldn’t send your code. Check your connection and try again. Nothing you typed was lost." Field errors say the fix: "That number looks a digit short."

Empty states

What this place is for, then the first step: "No photos yet. This is the album everyone shares. Add the first one and the rest will follow."
