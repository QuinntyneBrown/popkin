Foundation

Elevation

Soft, neutral shadows in black. Three levels and a focus halo; in dark they deepen.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark
  • Overview
  • Shadows
  • Surfaces
  • Z-order

Overview

Cards float a little (--shadow-1), the QR card and floating actions lift (--shadow-2), sheets cast upward (--shadow-3).

Shadows

--shadow-1
Cards, bubbles, secondary buttons
--shadow-2
QR card, floating action, toasts
--shadow-3
Sheets and dialogs
--shadow-focus
Focused field halo

Surfaces

Canvas, then surface (white or night card), then raised (sheets). In dark, surfaces get lighter as they rise and shadows get stronger.

Surface with --shadow-1
Surface with --shadow-1

Z-order

Sticky bars --z-sticky; scrim --z-overlay; dialogs --z-modal; toasts --z-toast. No raw z-index anywhere.
