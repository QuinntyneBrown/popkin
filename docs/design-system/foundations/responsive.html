Foundation

Responsive

Mobile first from 360px. The app grows from one column to three panes over five form factors; the landing page grows from one column to four.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark
  • Overview

  • Touch

Overview

Base styles are for 360px. min-width queries in rem add room. The app changes at 640 (S), 768 (M), 1024 (L) and 1280px (XL); the landing page at 576, 768 and 992px, and its navigation shows Sign in from 640px (L2-002 AC4).

Touch

  • Every target is at least 44px.
  • No hover-only affordance; press-and-hold has a keyboard equivalent (focus and Enter).
  • Sticky footers and tabs leave room for the home indicator.
  • Full-height screens use 100dvh.

Testing

Screenshot each screen at 360 × 800, 768 × 1024, 1024 × 800 and 1280 × 800 in both themes and check: no horizontal scroll, nothing clipped, sheets under 640px, controls still 44px.
