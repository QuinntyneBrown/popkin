Foundation

Layout

The app fills the screen on five form factors, from one column on a phone to three panes on a desktop. The landing page uses a 72rem container with 4, 8 and 12 columns.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark

Overview

The designs draw every app screen for five form factors: XS, S, M, L and XL. A phone gets one column and the tabs along the bottom. From M the tabs become a side rail and onboarding splits in two; from L the rail is a sidebar and a second pane opens; from XL a third. Forms keep a readable column (--layout-app-max, then --layout-form-max-s and --layout-form-max-l) and centre it in their half.

--layout-breakpoint-app-mM: the Popkin tabs become a side rail; onboarding, joining and wrapping up split in two.
--layout-breakpoint-app-lL: the rail becomes a sidebar; chat, the home, an experience and a photo open a second pane.
--layout-breakpoint-app-xlXL: a thread and a call open a third pane.

Media queries cannot read custom properties, so the same values are written in rem in the CSS.

Grid

Columns , gutter , margin at this width.

Screen templates

  • Step: top bar with step dots, title, content, sticky footer action (sign up, name, how long).
  • Tab root: tab header with title and filters, list, floating action, Popkin tabs (chat, photos, experiences).
  • Home: tinted header with the Popkin's identity and two actions, sections, tabs.
  • Thread: sticky top bar with a hairline, messages, sticky composer.
  • Stage: dark screen for calls and photos.
  • Washed: centred moment on the Popkin colour (invite, welcome, recap).
  • Split (from M): the form in one half, a picture of what it makes in the other (onboarding, joining, wrapping up).
  • Shell (from M): the Popkin's rail or sidebar beside any screen inside a Popkin.

Usage

  • No horizontal page scroll at 360px.
  • Sticky footers and tabs leave room for the home indicator (20 to 32px).
  • Prose is capped at --layout-measure.
