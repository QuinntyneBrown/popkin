Iconography

Lucide icons on a 24-unit grid with a 2px round stroke, in currentColor. Emoji are content, not icons.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark

Overview

Icons come from Lucide (ISC licence), the set the designs use, inlined as SVG. Each Popkin's emoji is its identity and appears in tiles, never as a UI icon.

Sizes

16px inline, 20px in buttons, 24px default, 28px for back chevrons (with a 2.4px stroke).

Usage

  • Icons sit beside a word, or inside an icon button with an aria-label.
  • Decorative SVGs carry aria-hidden="true".
  • One icon means one thing: the camera always adds a photo; the video camera always means a call.

Accessibility

Icons that carry meaning without a word (mic-off on a call tile) have hidden text beside them. Icons meet 3:1 against their background.
