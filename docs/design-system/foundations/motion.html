Foundation

Motion

Quick and springy: presses squash to 97%, sheets slide up, a new Popkin pops with a burst of confetti. All of it goes away with reduced motion.

Overview

Motion explains where something came from: sheets from the bottom, toasts from below, the tile springing in when a Popkin is born or someone joins.

Durations and easing

The pop

🏕️

When a Popkin is created or someone joins, the tile scales from 96% to 106% to 100% over 280ms and twelve dots burst once. Under reduced motion only the tile appears.

Usage

  • Nothing over 320ms except progress and the skeleton shimmer.
  • No motion on page load except the shimmer and the pop.
  • Use --ease-pop for things that respond to touch; --ease-standard for fades.

Accessibility

Under prefers-reduced-motion the duration tokens drop to 0.01ms and --scale-press to 1, so components need no extra queries. Nothing flashes.
