Foundation

Typography

Nunito for headings: rounded, warm, heavy weights only. Inter for everything people read and write, at 16px or larger. Rubik only in the wordmark.

WCAG 2.2 AALight and dark

Overview

Three families, all bundled under the SIL Open Font License in assets/fonts/ and declared in tokens.css, so nothing waits on a network. Body text is 16px Inter; the smallest text is 12px and only for times.

Usage

  • Headings balance (text-wrap: balance); paragraphs avoid widows (pretty).
  • Tight tracking (−0.02em) on display sizes only.
  • Numbers in counts and stats use tabular figures.
  • The half-steps (13, 15 and 17px) come from the design: metadata, list previews and inputs. They are named -sm-plus, -md-minus and -md-plus.

Accessibility

  • All sizes are in rem and follow the browser's text size.
  • Nothing below 12px; body never below 16px; inputs 17px so iOS does not zoom.
  • Line height 1.4 for UI, 1.6 for reading.
