Foundation

Color

Coral leads on white paper with black ink: the Straight palette. Sun and green for small moments of cheer, and seven colours a Popkin can make its own. Every role has a light and a dark value.

Overview

Colours have three tiers. Primitives (--palette-*) are the raw values. Roles (--color-*) say what a colour is for and change with the theme. Popkin colour (--accent*, on Theming) is the colour a Popkin picks, set with data-accent. Components use roles and the Popkin colour, never primitives.

Status

Info is sand and ink; success is green; warning is sun; danger is coral. Each has a background, a text, an icon, a border and a solid value; text always passes 4.5:1 on its background.

Usage

  • One coral primary per screen. Inside a Popkin, its own colour takes the main action instead.
  • Muted text (#3b3b3b) is for secondary lines; subtle text (#6b6b6b) for times and placeholders; #8f8f8f only for disabled text.
  • Coral, sun, green and every Popkin colour are light mid-tones: text and icons on them are black. As text or an edge on white they are darkened (--color-fg-accent, --accent-text, --accent-line) to reach 4.5:1 and 3:1.
  • Calls and the photo view use the black stage in both themes.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone: unread is bold and a dot; your reaction has an edge; a chosen card has a radio dot.
