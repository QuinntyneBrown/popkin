# Journeys

Ordered by value. Effort is days for an intermediate developer.

| # | Journey | Description | Effort (days) |
|---|---------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Start a Popkin | Visitor signs up with a code, starts a Popkin and gets its QR invite. | 10 |
| 2 | Join by invite | Invited person scans the QR, previews the Popkin, joins and is welcomed. | 5 |
| 3 | Chat with everyone | Members send and receive live messages in the shared thread. | 5 |
| 4 | Popkin home | Member sees people, time left, latest chat, photos and experiences. | 3 |
| 5 | Share photos | Members add photos, browse the album by day and view each one. | 5 |
| 6 | Come back | Member signs in again and opens any Popkin from My Popkins. | 3 |
| 7 | Group call | Member starts or joins a video call and controls mic and camera. | 8 |
| 8 | Rate an experience | Members share an experience, rate it, comment and see the group score. | 5 |
| 9 | Wrap up | Host or time ends the Popkin; it turns read-only with a recap. | 5 |
| 10 | Discover Popkin | Visitor reads the landing page and starts a Popkin or signs in. | 2 |
| 11 | Direct message | Member messages one member privately and calls them from the thread. | 3 |
| 12 | React and reply | Member reacts to a message with emoji or replies to it. | 3 |
| 13 | Photos in chat | Member attaches photos to a chat message. | 2 |
| 14 | Engage with a photo | Member reacts to, comments on and saves a photo. | 3 |
| 15 | Join by code | Person types an invite code to join without scanning. | 1 |
| 16 | Change Popkin settings | Host changes the Popkin's name, emoji, colour and length. | 2 |
| 17 | Manage account | Person changes their name and photo, or signs out. | 2 |
| 18 | Leave a Popkin | Member leaves a Popkin they no longer want. | 1 |
| 19 | Delete account | Person deletes their account and personal data. | 2 |
| 20 | Say keen | Member marks an experience the group wants to do as keen. | 1 |
| | **Total** | | **77** |
