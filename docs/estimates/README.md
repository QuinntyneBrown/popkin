## Roadmap

1. Wrap-up: wrap a Popkin up, which ends its call and stops "Share one" (journey 9).
2. Chat and calls: direct messages and the direct call (journey 11), reactions, replies and photos in chat (journeys 12 and 13).
3. Photos: react, comment, save and delete (journey 14), and the photo store of a deployed system.
4. Landing, join with a code, account management and keen (the rest of journeys 10 to 20).
5. Reliability: a saved answer for every screen and an app that opens without a connection.
