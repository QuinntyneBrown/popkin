# 25 · Journey 23: Switch a feature

## Hook

Plan and Schedules can be switched, for every Popkin at once.

## Page

Features shows each one, how far it reaches and since when: Plan on in every live Popkin, Schedules in three chosen ones.

## Off

An Owner chooses Off. The dialog says what happens: the tab disappears within a minute, and nothing is deleted.

A reason goes to the audit log.

## Done

Schedules is off: hidden in every Popkin, with who did it and when.

## Everyone

Everyone turns it on for all of them, again with a reason.

## Chosen

Or keep it to chosen Popkins. Add one by searching the live Popkins, and remove one with its cross.

## Support

A Support admin sees every switch, switched off: only Owners can do this.

## Grow

On a phone the panels stack. On a desktop, the sidebar sits beside them.

## Proof

What the tests prove: when a change fails, nothing changes and the reason is kept to try again.

## Close

That is journey twenty-three. Popkin is a free platform for pop-up communities.
