# 24 · Journey 24: Open the admin app

## Hook

Popkin has a console for the people who run it. This is how they get in.

## Signin

Lena opens the audit log, and the console asks who she is: her staff email first.

## Code

A code goes to that address, and it works for ten minutes.

## Audit

Then the page she asked for opens. The audit log lists every action taken in the console, newest first, with who did it.

It is kept for two years, and nobody can edit it.

## Admins

Admins and roles lists everyone who can sign in, with their role and when they were last active.

## Add

An Owner adds an admin with their popkin dot app address. They are invited until they first sign in with a code.

## Role

Changing a role says what changes, and asks for a reason, which goes into the audit log.

## Roles

What each role can do sets Owner, Support and Viewer side by side.

## Grow

On a phone it is one column. On a desktop, a sidebar names the pages.

## Proof

What the tests prove: a right code that is not an admin's is turned away, and the console always keeps one Owner.

## Close

That is journey twenty-four. Popkin is a free platform for pop-up communities.
