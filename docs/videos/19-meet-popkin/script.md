# 19 · Meet Popkin

The narration, scene by scene. The scenes' lengths and the voice are in `narration.json`; this file
is the transcript.

## hook

You know the moment. Someone says: we should all get together.

## problem

Then the group chat begins. Numbers added one by one, photos lost in the scroll, plans buried under
replies.

## meet

Meet Popkin. A private space for your people, popped up in seconds, and free.

## invite

Hold up the QR code. Friends scan it, add their name, and they're in. No app to download.

## chat

Chat with everyone. React, reply, or slip into a direct message.

## photos

Every photo lands in one shared album, by the day, from everyone.

## call

Can't all be in one place? One tap, and the whole group is on a call.

## rate

Share what you did, and what you're keen to do next. Rate it together, and see the group's score.

## wrap

And when it's over, it doesn't just fade out. You get a proper send-off: a recap, the best moment,
and the album to keep.

## free

Free. Like, actually free. No ads, no premium tier. It works in the browser, on a phone or a
desktop.

## close

Popkin. Your people, popped up in seconds. Start one tonight.
