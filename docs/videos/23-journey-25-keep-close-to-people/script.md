# 23 · Journey 25: Keep close to people

## Hook

A Popkin ends. The people in it should not drift away. That is what Contacts is for.

## Today

Every morning, Today leads with what matters: Grace's birthday on Saturday, what she is into, and the last thing you talked about.

Beside it, what to do today, and who it has been a while since.

## Add

Add someone with a name, and as much else as you know. Only you can see them.

## Profile

Their profile keeps it together: how to reach Chidi, your habit with him, and what he loves.

## Log

Log a conversation, and anything you promised becomes a follow-up.

## Memories

Keep memories with them: photos, files and notes.

## Dates

Dates gathers every birthday and anniversary, soonest first. Follow-ups lists what you said you would do.

## Habits

Habits keep a rhythm: lunch with Chidi every two weeks, and a nudge when it slips.

## Connections

Connections draws who knows whom, family, friends and work. Groups gather people, and start a Popkin with them.

## Import

And Import brings in everyone you share a Popkin with, once each. They are not told.

## Grow

On a phone, one column. On a desktop, the tabs become a sidebar.

## Proof

What the tests prove: reminders arrive at nine in the morning where you are, and nobody you add is ever told.

## Close

That is journey twenty-five. Popkin is a free platform for pop-up communities.
